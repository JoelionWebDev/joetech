Start with a landing page and a WhatsApp group. Build a one-page site explaining the idea, add a 'Join the waitlist' form, and run small Instagram ads to tailors and customers for two weeks. If 100 real people join, build a simple version. Also talk to 10 tailors in person — if they will not join a free WhatsApp group, they will not use a paid app. The waitlist is the proof; build only after it exists.
