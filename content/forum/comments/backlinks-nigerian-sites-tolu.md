Start with the safe ones: the business's own social profiles, its Google Business Profile, and real Nigerian business directories like VConnect or Finelib — not the spammy ones. Then get links from partners: the supplier, the estate agent, the event hall that recommends the business. One link from a real local partner is worth more than 50 directory links. Never buy links — Google catches it and the site drops.
