Customers forgive a slow reply if you set the expectation first. An away message that says "Thanks for messaging, we reply between 9am and 6pm" plus a link to your catalogue keeps most people patient until morning.

What loses customers is silence with no message at all — they assume the business is closed for good and move to the next shop. Even a short auto-reply with your opening hours is better than nothing.
