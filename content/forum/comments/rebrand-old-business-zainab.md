Roll it out in phases instead of changing everything overnight. Keep the pink and brown as an accent colour in the new design so regulars still recognise the bakery, change the shop sign and bags first, and put a small note on the counter explaining the fresh look. Customers accept change when they feel included in it, not surprised by it.
