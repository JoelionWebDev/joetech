Avoid mega menus and big images inside the menu — they are heavy on slow networks and most visitors never open them. Use a simple hamburger with the phone number written next to it, and make the menu close after one tap on a link. I also lazy-load anything below the fold so the menu itself opens instantly even on MTN 3G.
