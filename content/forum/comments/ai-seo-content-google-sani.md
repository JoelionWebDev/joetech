Google punishes thin, unhelpful content — AI or human. Use AI to draft, then add what only a human can: real prices, real Lagos examples, real photos, and answers from actual customers. Edit every post so it sounds like a person who knows the business. One genuinely helpful post a week beats 20 generic AI posts, and it is safer. Tell the client the goal is calls and customers, not post count.
