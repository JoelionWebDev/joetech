Do not throw the old logo away on day one. Keep it on receipts and the old Facebook page cover for a month while the new one takes over, and update the Google Business Profile photos gradually so search still finds the bakery. Announce the rebrand on WhatsApp status with a photo of the owner and the new sign — regulars trust the face more than the logo.
