Placement matters as much as the words. A testimonial hidden on a separate page does nothing — put one or two short ones right next to the price or the order button, where the customer is already deciding.

Short video clips work best, even 20 seconds on a phone. And always use the person's real first name and business if they agree; "Happy Customer, Lagos" convinces nobody. Ask for the testimonial the same week you deliver the work, while the excitement is still fresh.
