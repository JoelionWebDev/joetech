Do not fight it — meet the customer where they are. Make the website a beautiful menu and price list with a WhatsApp button on every product, so the tap sends the product name straight into the chat. The site does the selling, WhatsApp closes the sale. Tell the owner the website is the shop window and WhatsApp is the counter. When she is ready, add Paystack checkout later — but only after the WhatsApp flow is making money.
