From a branding angle, the catalogue only works if it looks like the same business everywhere. Use the same colours, the same style of photos and the same business name as your shop sign and your flyers — customers trust what looks consistent and get suspicious when every place looks different.

For quick orders from people who already know you, the catalogue is faster than a website. But a website still wins for new customers finding you on Google. If you can only afford one, start with the catalogue, then add a simple website when sales grow.
