As a developer, I love it when a client hands me even a short brand guide. It saves three rounds of "change the colour, change the font" because the decisions are already made.

My five pages would be: logo and how to use it, two main colours with their codes, one or two fonts, the tone of voice for writing, and examples of what NOT to do. One page each, no long grammar. If it fits in a PDF you can send on WhatsApp, it is the right size.
