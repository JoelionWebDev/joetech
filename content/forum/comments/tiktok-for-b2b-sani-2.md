TikTok works for B2B if you teach, not sell. Short videos like 'How to spot a fake printing machine' or 'Why your prints fade in 3 months' reach the exact people who buy machines — they are on TikTok too, just not in work mode. Put the business WhatsApp number in the bio and answer every comment. One of my clients got 3 real enquiries from a single 40-second video about maintenance. Try 8 videos before you judge it.
