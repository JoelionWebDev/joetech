For small business sites I use Formspree — free tier handles the volume most traders need, and the submissions land in email so the owner never logs into anything new. The trick is the fallback: if the form service is down or the customer has bad network, there must be a WhatsApp button right under the form. Half my clients' customers will tap WhatsApp before they finish typing a form anyway. Also validate on the page itself — a phone number field that accepts letters will waste the owner's time.
