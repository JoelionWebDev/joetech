I use both, but never for the same job. AI images are fine for backgrounds, mockups and hero banners where no real photo exists. For the business itself — the owner, the shop, the food — real photos win every time. Customers in Onitsha Main Market can spot a fake face in one second, and once they doubt one photo they doubt the whole business. My rule: real photos of the people and products, AI only for decoration around them.
