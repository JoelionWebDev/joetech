Before I write a line of code, I build a one-page landing page and a WhatsApp number and see if strangers actually ask for it. If ten real people will not leave their number or pay a small deposit, the idea is not ready yet. I learnt this the hard way — I built a full app once that only my friends downloaded. Talk to the market first, then build the smallest version that solves one problem.
