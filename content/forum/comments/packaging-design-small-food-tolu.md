Start with the label, not the pack. For chin-chin the label must carry the product name, weight, ingredients, expiry date and your contact — and a NAFDAC number if the business is registered. The cheapest clean route is sticker labels on plain packs: one strong colour, a big readable product name, and one appetite photo. Print a small batch first and watch how it looks on the stall next to other products before you commit to a big print run.
