Real photos also convert better, from what I have seen on client sites. A phone photo taken in good daylight of the actual shop or product builds more trust than a perfect AI image that looks like it came from another country. You do not need a professional camera — clean the lens, stand near a window, take ten shots and pick the best two. Save the AI images for blog headers and social posts where the photo is not the product itself.
