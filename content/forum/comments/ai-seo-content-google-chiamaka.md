Use AI for the outline and first draft, then rewrite it in the client's own voice with real examples from their business. Google's problem is not AI, it is thin content that says nothing new — add the client's prices, photos of real work, and answers to questions customers actually ask on WhatsApp. Twenty generic posts will do less than five useful ones.
