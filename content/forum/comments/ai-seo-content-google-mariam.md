From an SEO view, the safe way is to treat AI as a starting point and edit heavily: check every fact, add local details only a human in Nigeria would know, and link to the client's real service pages. Publish fewer posts but make each one genuinely helpful, and watch Search Console for two weeks after publishing — if clicks drop, that topic needs a human rewrite, not more AI output.
