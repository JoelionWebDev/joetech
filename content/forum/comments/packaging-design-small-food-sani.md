Keep the design simple on purpose. A market stall is noisy — the customer has two seconds. Big product name, one clear photo of the chin-chin, and the price where the eye lands first. I would also ask the printer for a sample of the exact paper before the full run, because cheap sticker paper can make colours look washed out. One round of design is enough if the brief is tight: agree the label size and the exact words with the seller before the designer starts.
