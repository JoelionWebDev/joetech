Change it in stages, not overnight. Keep the name and one familiar colour, and refresh the logo shape and the second colour. Put up a small sign in the shop: 'Same bakery, fresh new look', and use the new branding on bags and boxes first, where customers take it home. Run both side by side for a month. Customers follow the name and the taste, not the exact shade of pink — but they need to see the connection.
