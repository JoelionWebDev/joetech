Price it as a package, not per design. A starter kit — profile picture, 5 highlight covers, 10 templates and a price list — is fair at 50,000 to 80,000 naira in Lagos, depending on how custom it is. Show the list of everything included before you quote, so the client sees the value. And deliver the templates in Canva as well as the finished files, so she can reuse them — that one extra is what makes clients come back.
