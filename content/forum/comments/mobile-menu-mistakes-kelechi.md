The worst mistake is burying the phone number and WhatsApp button inside the menu. Put a sticky call button at the bottom of the screen so it is always visible, keep the menu to five items at most, and test it on a cheap Android phone with 3G data, not just your own phone. If it takes more than two taps to call the business, customers will leave.
