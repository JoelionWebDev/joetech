It works, but only if you teach instead of selling. The B2B decision-makers are on TikTok too, just scrolling after work. A 40-second video like "how we built this booking site for a salon" or "three mistakes in this shop's checkout" gets watched because it is useful. The moment it sounds like an advert, people swipe. Start with one useful video a week and watch which ones get saved — saves are the signal that business owners found it worth keeping.
