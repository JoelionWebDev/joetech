The printer is right that hex codes are screen-only — they mean nothing to a printing press. A brand guide should carry the CMYK values and, if you can, a Pantone reference for each brand colour, plus a printed swatch the client has actually seen and approved. Ask the printer for one test print of the flyer before the full run; it costs little and it ends the argument about whose fault the dull green is. Once the client signs the proof, the colour is settled.
