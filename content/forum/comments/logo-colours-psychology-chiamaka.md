Colours matter, but not in the way clients think. On Instagram and WhatsApp, your logo is tiny on a phone screen — if it does not read clearly at that size, the psychology does not get a chance to work. I always test a logo in one colour first, then in black and white. If it still looks like the brand, the colour choice is doing its job. Red can work for food and energy, but if the client prints on cheap banners, a bright red turns orange in the sun, so pick a shade that survives print.
