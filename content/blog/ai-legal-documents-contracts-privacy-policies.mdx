Ask a Nigerian business owner what they have been avoiding, and "writing a proper contract" or "updating my website's privacy policy" will be near the top of the list. It is easy to understand why. Legal documents feel like a foreign language, lawyers can be expensive, and a template found online is usually written for another country entirely.

The result is that many businesses run on handshake agreements and privacy policies that were copied from somewhere and never reviewed.

AI can change that. A well-guided AI can produce a strong first draft of a service contract, a privacy policy, or terms of service in minutes. It will not replace a lawyer — and you must be honest about that. But it removes the excuse that "I don't know where to start." Here is how to do it safely.

## What AI Is Genuinely Good At

- **First drafts from scratch.** Describe your business and the relationship, and AI produces a complete, organised document in minutes.
- **Plain-language summaries.** Ask it to explain any clause in your draft in simple English so you know what you are agreeing to.
- **Comparing options.** Ask "What are three ways to structure this payment clause and what does each protect?"
- **Catching gaps.** Ask "What common clauses are missing from a Nigerian service contract?" and let it expand your draft.
- **Consistency.** Draft contracts, privacy policies, and NDAs from the same set of business facts so nothing contradicts itself.

## What AI Cannot Do

This is the part that keeps everyone out of trouble.

- **It is not licensed to practise law.** In Nigeria, only licensed legal practitioners can give legal advice. AI gives you drafts and information, not advice tailored to your specific legal position.
- **It cannot know your full legal situation.** Contracts sit inside regulations, tax obligations, and industry rules an AI does not see.
- **It does not know the latest law.** AI models have a knowledge cutoff and can describe outdated law confidently.
- **It cannot make court-valid decisions for you.** What works in an informal setting may fail in a Nigerian court because of procedural requirements.

The practical rule: use AI to prepare, but have a professional review anything that matters.

## The Documents Businesses Most Often Need

### Service contracts and agreements

Whenever you take money for a service — web design, consulting, delivery, maintenance — a written agreement protects both sides. The draft should cover the scope of work, price and payment schedule, what happens on late payment, timelines, confidentiality, and what happens if either party wants to end the relationship.

### Privacy policy

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and the NDPR, businesses handling personal data have obligations to tell people how their data is used. If your website collects names, emails, or payment details, a privacy policy is not optional decoration — it is a compliance requirement.

### Terms of service

This is the rulebook for how people use your website or app: acceptable use, intellectual property, refunds, disclaimers, and governing law.

### Non-disclosure agreements

An NDA protects your business ideas and information when you talk to potential partners, investors, or contractors.

## A Safe Drafting Workflow

### Step 1: Prepare your business facts first

Before you ask for anything, gather the facts: your business name and registration details, your service description, your prices, your refund policy, your location, and how you collect data. Complete, accurate facts produce complete, accurate drafts.

### Step 2: Write a specific prompt

Generic prompts give generic documents. A strong prompt looks like this:

> Draft a service agreement for my business. Business name: (name). I provide (service) to (type of client) in Nigeria. Price and payment terms: (details). It should cover scope, payment, late payment, timelines, confidentiality, termination, and dispute resolution. Use clear, plain language. Flag any clause that a lawyer should review carefully.

### Step 3: Review line by line, and ask questions

Read the draft and ask the AI to explain every clause you are unsure about. You should be able to summarise the entire document in your own words. If you cannot, you are not ready to use it.

### Step 4: Get professional review for anything important

If this contract protects a significant transaction, or if you have employees, customers' personal data, or substantial money at stake, pay a lawyer to review your AI draft. Think of it as hiring the professional to check the AI's work — a much cheaper route than starting from zero.

## Red Flags to Watch For

- **Copying a foreign template blindly.** Law varies by country. Your draft must reference Nigerian context where relevant.
- **Signing what you have not read.** If you cannot explain a clause, do not sign it.
- **Believing AI output is automatically compliant.** Compliance requires applying the current law to your specific facts.
- **Skipping review on regulated industries.** If your business is in finance, health, education, or anything licensed, professional review is not optional.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I just use an AI-generated contract to avoid paying for a lawyer?**

You can draft and use it for low-stakes matters, but you accept the risk. For anything significant, review by a professional is the responsible choice. The AI saves you time and money on the drafting; the lawyer protects you on the risk.

**Is a privacy policy required even if my website is small?**

If you collect any personal data — even a contact form — Nigerian data protection law applies. Requirements scale with your processing, but "I'm small" does not mean "I am exempt."

**How do I update my existing privacy policy?**

Use AI to review your current policy against your actual practices. Ask: "Does this policy accurately describe how my website collects and uses data?" Fix the gaps, then update the document with a new effective date.

**Should AI-drafted contracts mention Nigerian law?**

Yes. Include a governing law and jurisdiction clause appropriate to your situation and have it reviewed before relying on it.
