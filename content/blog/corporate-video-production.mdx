Corporate videos are professional video content created for business purposes. They are different from social media videos or advertising content. They are designed to communicate your brand message in a polished, professional format.

Corporate videos serve multiple purposes — attracting clients, recruiting talent, training employees, communicating with stakeholders, and documenting your company's story.

Here is what Nigerian businesses need to know about corporate video production.

## Types of Corporate Videos

A brand video tells your company story. It covers your mission, values, history, and vision. Brand videos are used on your website homepage, at events, and in presentations. They typically run two to three minutes.

A company culture video shows what it is like to work at your organization. These videos help attract talent and showcase your workplace environment. They feature real employees, real workspaces, and genuine interactions.

A product or service video explains what you offer and how it benefits customers. These are more polished versions of product demo videos. They are suitable for your website, trade shows, and sales presentations.

A training video teaches employees how to perform specific tasks. Training videos reduce the need for repeated in-person training and ensure consistency. They are used internally and distributed through your learning management system.

An event video documents a company event — a launch, conference, anniversary celebration, or team building activity. Event videos capture the energy and atmosphere of live events.

A testimonial video features satisfied customers sharing their positive experiences. Corporate testimonials are professionally produced versions of customer stories.

## The Production Process

Pre-production is the planning phase. This includes defining the video's purpose and target audience, writing the script, creating a storyboard, planning the shoot with location scouting, scheduling, and casting, and assembling the production team.

Production is the filming phase. Depending on the video type, production may include studio filming with controlled lighting and background, location filming at your office, factory, or customer site, interview filming with professional audio setup, and B-roll filming of your operations, products, and team.

Post-production is the editing phase. This includes reviewing and selecting the best takes, editing the footage into a coherent narrative, adding music, graphics, and color grading, incorporating voiceover if needed, and delivering the final video in required formats.

## Investment Required

Corporate video costs vary significantly based on complexity. A simple interview-style video with one camera and basic editing costs less than a multi-location production with animation and professional actors.

Script development costs are minimal but essential. A good script ensures your message is clear and effective.

Production costs include crew (camera operator, sound technician, director), equipment (cameras, lighting, audio), and location costs. Multi-day shoots cost more than single-day shoots.

Post-production costs include editing, color grading, sound design, music licensing, and motion graphics or animation.

Distribution costs are separate from production. Hosting on your website, promoting on social media, and running ad campaigns all require separate budgets.

## Using Corporate Videos Effectively

Your website homepage should feature a brand video that communicates your value proposition within seconds. Visitors who watch a brand video are more likely to explore your site and contact you.

Your About page benefits from a company culture video. Prospective clients and employees want to know who they would be working with.

Sales presentations are more effective with a product or service video. Video communicates faster than a verbal explanation and ensures consistent messaging across your sales team.

Social media profiles benefit from shorter versions of your corporate videos. A thirty-second cut of your brand video works as a LinkedIn or Instagram profile introduction.
