Influencer marketing is no longer just for big brands with big budgets. In 2026, with social media algorithms prioritising authentic content over polished production, micro-influencers (1,000-50,000 followers) often deliver better engagement and ROI than celebrity influencers — at a fraction of the cost.

Here is how to run effective influencer marketing campaigns on a budget.

## Why Micro-Influencers?

- **Higher engagement** — Micro-influencers have 3-6x higher engagement rates than macro-influencers
- **More authentic** — Followers trust them as peers, not celebrities
- **Lower cost** — ₦10,000-100,000 per post vs. ₦500,000+ for macro-influencers
- **Better targeting** — They have specific, engaged niches
- **More personal relationships** — They read and respond to comments

## Finding the Right Micro-Influencers

### Criteria

- **Relevance** — Their content aligns with your brand
- **Engagement rate** — 3%+ is good, 5%+ is excellent
- **Audience demographics** — Do their followers match your target audience?
- **Content quality** — Does their style fit your brand aesthetic?
- **Authenticity** — Do they genuinely use products they promote?

### Where to Search

- Instagram search (relevant hashtags and locations)
- TikTok discovery page
- YouTube niche communities
- LinkedIn for B2B influencers
- Influencer platforms (Upfluence, AspireIQ)

## Reaching Out

### The Pitch

Keep it personal and professional:

1. Compliment their content (be specific)
2. Explain why they are a good fit for your brand
3. Describe what you are offering (free product, payment, affiliate commission)
4. Make it easy to say yes

### Compensation

Options for working with micro-influencers:
- Free product + affiliate commission (good for early-stage)
- Flat fee (₦20,000-100,000 per post)
- Performance-based (commission on sales)
- Product gifting (for very small influencers)

## Campaign Design

### Brief

Provide a clear campaign brief:
- Key messaging points
- Dos and don'ts
- Posting requirements (format, timing, hashtags)
- Disclosure requirements (#ad or #sponsored)

### Creative Freedom

Let influencers create content that feels authentic to their style. Influencers know their audience better than you do.

## Measuring Success

Track:
- Engagement (likes, comments, shares)
- Reach and impressions
- Website clicks / profile visits
- Discount code usage
- Sales attributed to the campaign

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many followers should a micro-influencer have?**

1,000-50,000 followers is the sweet spot. Engagement rate matters more than follower count.

**Should I pay micro-influencers or just send free products?**

Free products work for nano-influencers (under 1,000 followers). Micro-influencers (1,000-50,000) typically expect payment. Offer both: product + fee.

**How do I measure ROI from influencer marketing?**

Use unique discount codes, UTM links, and track sales. Calculate: (Revenue generated - Campaign cost) / Campaign cost.
