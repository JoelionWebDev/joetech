If you run a business from your phone, you know the problem. Your personal chats and your customer chats live in the same WhatsApp. A customer messages you at 11 pm while you are talking to family. A personal group chat buries an important order. You want to switch off work for the evening, but you cannot, because the same app holds both lives.

The solution is two WhatsApp accounts on one phone: one number for business, one for personal. And you no longer need two phones, a cloned app, or a risky trick to do it. WhatsApp now supports two accounts inside the same app, and there are safe official ways to add a third. This guide shows you how.

## Method 1: Two Accounts Inside WhatsApp (The Official Way)

Since October 2023, the WhatsApp app on Android lets you sign in to two accounts and switch between them. The same feature reached iPhone in 2026. This is the cleanest method, because it uses the official app and nothing else.

You need two things: a second phone number, and the ability to receive a verification code on it. The second number can be a second SIM card in a dual-SIM phone, or an eSIM. WhatsApp ties an account to a phone number, not to a SIM slot, so as long as the number can receive the six-digit code by SMS or call, it works.

Here is how to set it up on Android:

1. Open WhatsApp and tap the three dots, then **Settings**.
2. Tap the small arrow next to your name at the top.
3. Tap **Add account**.
4. Tap **Agree and continue**, then enter your second phone number with the country code.
5. Enter the six-digit code WhatsApp sends to that number.
6. Set your profile name and photo for the second account.

To switch between accounts, tap the three dots, then **Switch accounts**, and choose the other one. Each account keeps its own chats, calls, notifications, privacy settings, and profile photo. They are completely separate — a message sent to one account never appears in the other.

On iPhone, the steps are similar: open WhatsApp, go to **Settings**, tap the arrow next to your name, and choose **Add account**. If you do not see the option, update WhatsApp from the App Store first, because the feature rolled out gradually.

A few things to know about this method: you can have a maximum of two accounts inside the one app. Each account needs its own two-step verification PIN. And you set up the second account on your main phone — you cannot add a second account on a linked device like a tablet or a computer.

## Method 2: WhatsApp Business as Your Second Account

WhatsApp Business is a separate, official app from Meta, made for businesses. It installs alongside regular WhatsApp, so you can run one number on WhatsApp and a second number on WhatsApp Business. That gives you two accounts in two apps on the same phone.

This method is a good choice if your second number is for business, because WhatsApp Business adds useful tools: a business profile with your address and hours, a catalogue to show your products, quick replies for messages you send often, automated greeting messages, and labels to organise your chats.

To set it up, download **WhatsApp Business** from the Play Store or App Store, open it, and register your business number with the six-digit code. If you are moving a number from regular WhatsApp to WhatsApp Business, your chats can move with you — the app will offer to transfer them during setup. Our guide on [setting up a WhatsApp Business catalogue](/blog/whatsapp-business-catalogue-account-guide) walks through the business features in detail.

You can also combine the two methods: two accounts inside regular WhatsApp, plus a third number on WhatsApp Business. That is the most accounts you can run safely with official apps.

## Method 3: Your Phone's Built-In Dual App Feature

Many Android phones have a built-in feature that runs two copies of the same app. Samsung calls it **Dual Messenger**. Xiaomi calls it **Dual Apps**. Other brands have their own names, such as App Twin or Clone App. It does the same job: it creates a second copy of WhatsApp on your phone, and you register your second number in the copy.

On a Samsung phone, the steps are:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Advanced features**, then **Dual Messenger**.
2. Find WhatsApp in the list and switch it on.
3. A second WhatsApp icon appears on your home screen, marked with a small badge.
4. Open the second WhatsApp and register your second number.

On a Xiaomi phone, go to **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **Dual Apps**, and switch on WhatsApp. Other brands are similar — search your settings for "dual" or "clone".

This method is safe because it uses your phone's own system, not a third-party app. The drawback is that you now have two WhatsApp icons to manage, and notifications come from two apps. It is a good option if the built-in multi-account feature is not available on your phone, or if you prefer your two accounts in two separate apps.

## What to Avoid: Unofficial WhatsApp Copies

You will see apps and websites offering "GB WhatsApp", "FM WhatsApp", and other modified versions that promise extra features. Do not use them.

These are not made by Meta. They can steal your messages, your contacts, and your account. WhatsApp regularly bans accounts that use them, which means you can lose your number, your chats, and your business contacts overnight, with no way to get them back. The official methods above do everything you need. There is no reason to risk your business on an unofficial copy.

## Keeping Two Accounts Organised

Running two accounts only helps if you keep them separate. A few habits make it work:

**Use different profile photos.** Give each account a clearly different photo — your face on one, your business logo on the other. That way you always know which account you are looking at before you send a message or post a status.

**Set different notification sounds.** Give your business account a different notification tone from your personal one. Then you know, without looking at the phone, whether a message is work or personal.

**Set business hours for yourself.** The point of a second account is that you can switch it off. Mute the business account in the evening if you need to rest. Customers can wait until morning, and you will answer them better when you are rested.

**Back up both accounts separately.** Each account has its own backup. Check that both are backing up to Google Drive or iCloud. Losing your business chats because only your personal account was backing up is a painful mistake. If you are moving to a new phone, our guide on [how to move your WhatsApp chats to a new phone](/blog/how-to-move-whatsapp-chats-new-phone-without-losing-anything) covers the transfer step by step.

**Turn on two-step verification on both accounts.** Go to Settings, then Account, then Two-step verification, and set a six-digit PIN for each account. That PIN stops someone from registering your number on another phone, even if they have your SIM card.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need two SIM cards?

You need two phone numbers that can receive a verification code. In practice, that usually means a dual-SIM phone or an eSIM. The number does not have to stay in your phone forever — WhatsApp only needs the number to receive the code during setup — but if you ever need to verify again, you will need access to that number.

### Can I use the same phone number for two WhatsApp accounts?

No. One phone number can only be registered to one WhatsApp account at a time. If you try to register the same number again, it will move the account, not copy it. You need a second number for a second account.

### Will my chats mix together?

No. Each account has its own chats, calls, status, and settings. Switching accounts is like opening a different app. A customer message on your business account will never appear in your personal chats.

### Does this work on iPhone?

Yes. WhatsApp added two-account support to iPhone in 2026. Update WhatsApp from the App Store, then look for the Add account option in Settings. If you have an older iPhone or an older version of WhatsApp, use WhatsApp Business as your second account instead — that works on every iPhone.

### Can I have three WhatsApp accounts on one phone?

Yes, by combining methods: two accounts inside the regular WhatsApp app, plus a third number on WhatsApp Business. That is the practical limit with official apps, and it is more than most people need.

### Will my contacts know I have two accounts?

Only if you tell them. Your contacts see whichever number they already have saved. A customer who has your business number sees your business profile. A friend who has your personal number sees your personal profile. The two accounts are invisible to each other.
