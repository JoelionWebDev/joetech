Screen recording is one of the most useful things your phone can do, and most people never use it. With it, you can show a customer exactly how to place an order on your website, record a video call with a client, save a live video you are watching, or create a quick tutorial for your staff without writing a long message. Instead of explaining something in ten paragraphs on WhatsApp, you record your screen once and send the video.

The good news is that both Android phones and iPhones now have screen recording built in. You do not need to download a separate app or pay anything. The button is already on your phone — it is just hidden in the quick settings or the control centre. This guide shows you where to find it, how to record with sound, how to edit the video, and how to fix the common problems people run into.

## Screen Record on Android

Most Android phones running Android 11 or newer have a built-in screen recorder. Samsung, Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi, Google Pixel and most other brands include it. If your phone is a few years old and runs Android 10 or older, the steps may be slightly different, but many brands added their own recorder even before Android 11, so it is worth checking first.

### Find the Screen Recorder

Swipe down from the top of your screen twice to open the full quick settings panel. You will see a grid of round or square icons — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Torch, Aeroplane Mode and others. Look for one labelled "Screen record" or "Screen recorder". On Samsung phones it is called "Screen recorder". On Tecno and Infinix phones it may be called "Screen record" or appear under a similar name.

If you cannot see it, swipe left on the quick settings panel to see the second page of icons. If it is still not there, tap the small pencil or edit icon, usually at the bottom or top of the panel, and you will see a list of extra tiles you can add. Drag the Screen record tile into your active quick settings, tap Done, and it will be there next time.

### Start Recording

Before you record, close any apps you do not want in the video and tidy up your home screen if you plan to show it. Turn on Do Not Disturb if you do not want notifications popping up in the middle of your recording — a WhatsApp message preview in your video can be embarrassing, especially if it shows a private chat.

Tap the Screen record tile. A small window will appear asking what you want to record. You will usually see options for sound: "No sound", "Media sounds" or "Media sounds and mic". Choose "Media sounds" if you want to capture the sound from a video or app playing on the phone. Choose "Media sounds and mic" if you want to talk over the recording while it is happening, for example when you are explaining something to a customer. Choose "No sound" if the audio does not matter.

Tap "Start recording". Your phone will usually count down from three, then a small floating button or a red dot appears at the top of your screen to show that recording has started. Now do whatever you want to show — open the app, tap through the steps, scroll slowly so viewers can follow. Speak clearly if you are narrating.

When you are finished, tap the floating stop button, or swipe down the notification shade and tap the "Stop" button on the screen recording notification. Your video is saved automatically, usually in your Photos or Gallery app, inside a folder called "Screen recordings" or "Movies".

### Record on Tecno, Infinix and Other Android Phones

Tecno and Infinix phones, which are very common in Nigeria, have a built-in recorder in the quick settings just like other Android phones. The name may be "Screen record". The steps are the same: swipe down twice, find the tile, choose your sound option, and tap start. Some models also have a screen recorder inside the "HiOS" or "XOS" toolbox, or in the Settings app under "Special Function" or a similar menu. If you cannot find the tile, open Settings and search for "screen record" in the search bar at the top — the phone will take you straight to it.

Xiaomi phones have a recorder called "Screen Recorder" that you can also find as an app in the app drawer, under the Tools folder. Opening the app lets you change settings such as video quality and frame rate before you record. Google Pixel phones use the standard Android recorder in the quick settings.

### Change the Quality and Save Location

By default, most Android phones record at a good quality that is fine for WhatsApp and social media. If your videos look blurry, you can raise the quality in the recorder settings. Open the screen recorder settings by long-pressing the Screen record tile in quick settings, or by opening Settings and searching for "screen recorder". Look for "Video quality" and choose 1080p if your phone supports it. Higher quality means a bigger file, so if you plan to send the video on WhatsApp, send it as a document instead of as a video to keep the quality.

## Screen Record on iPhone

iPhones have had a built-in screen recorder since iOS 11, so almost every iPhone in use today can do it. The button lives in the Control Centre, but it is not always switched on by default, so the first step is to add it.

### Add Screen Recording to Control Centre

Open the Settings app, tap "Control Centre", and scroll down to the list headed "More Controls". Find "Screen Recording" in that list and tap the green plus sign beside it. It will move up into the "Included Controls" list. That is all you need to do — the button is now in your Control Centre.

To open Control Centre, swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen on iPhones with Face ID, or swipe up from the bottom edge on older iPhones with a Home button. You will see the screen recording button: a circle with a dot inside it.

### Start Recording

Open Control Centre and tap the screen recording button once. Your phone counts down from three, the button turns red, and recording begins. The red bar or red dot at the top of your screen tells you it is recording. Now go and do whatever you want to capture.

To record with sound from your microphone — your voice explaining what you are doing — press and hold the screen recording button in Control Centre instead of tapping it once. A small menu appears with a "Microphone" option. Tap it to switch the microphone on, then tap "Start Recording". This is the step most people miss, and it is why many iPhone screen recordings come out silent. If you only want the sound from a video playing on the phone, you do not need the microphone; the phone records the internal audio automatically in most cases.

When you are finished, tap the red bar or red dot at the top of the screen and tap "Stop", or open Control Centre and tap the red recording button again. Your video is saved automatically in the Photos app, in your library and in the "Screen Recordings" album.

### Edit Your Recording on iPhone

You do not need a separate editing app for simple cuts. Open the video in the Photos app, tap "Edit" at the bottom, and you will see a timeline at the bottom of the screen. Drag the yellow handles at each end to trim off the beginning or end — for example, the three-second countdown or the moment you fumbled to open the right app. Tap "Done" and choose "Save Video" to replace the original or "Save Video as New Clip" to keep both.

## Record With Sound, the Right Way

Sound is where most screen recordings go wrong. Here are the rules that keep it simple.

If you are recording a video or a voice note that is already playing on your phone, choose the media sound option on Android, or just record normally on iPhone — the phone captures the sound coming from the app. If you are talking over the recording, switch the microphone on. On Android, choose "Media sounds and mic". On iPhone, long-press the recording button and turn the microphone on before you start.

Record in a quiet place. Background noise from a generator, traffic or a television will be picked up by the microphone and makes your recording hard to follow. If you are recording a tutorial for customers, find a quiet room and speak slowly and clearly.

One warning: some apps do not allow their audio to be recorded. Banking apps, Netflix and some other streaming apps block recording of their content for copyright or security reasons. If your screen goes black or the video has no sound while a particular app is open, that app is blocking the recording. There is no built-in way around this, and that is by design.

## Fix Common Screen Recording Problems

### The screen record button is missing

On Android, the tile may be on the second page of quick settings, or it may need to be added through the edit (pencil) icon. On some older phones, the recorder does not exist at all. If your phone runs Android 10 or older and has no recorder, you can download a free screen recorder from the Google Play Store — AZ Screen Recorder and XRecorder are two popular options. Check the reviews and the developer name before installing any app, and avoid apps that ask for unnecessary permissions.

On iPhone, if the button is not in Control Centre, add it from Settings, then Control Centre, as described above. If it is there but does nothing when you tap it, restart your phone and try again.

### The recording has no sound

This is the most common complaint. On Android, check that you chose a sound option that includes audio — "No sound" is sometimes selected by default. On iPhone, remember that a single tap records without the microphone; you need to long-press and switch the microphone on. Also check that the app you recorded allows its audio to be captured, and check your phone's volume: if the media volume is turned down, there is nothing to record.

### The video is too big to send on WhatsApp

Screen recordings, especially long ones at high quality, can be very large. WhatsApp has a limit on videos sent as videos. The simple fix is to send the file as a document: in WhatsApp, tap the attachment button, choose "Document", and select your video. It will send at full quality. Alternatively, trim the video to remove dead time at the start and end, or lower the recording quality in the settings before your next recording.

### The recording is upside down or sideways

This happens when the phone's orientation changes during recording. Lock your screen orientation before you start: on Android, turn on "Auto-rotate" only if you want it, or set the phone in the orientation you want and leave it there. On iPhone, use the orientation lock in Control Centre. The simplest approach is to hold the phone the way you want the final video to look and not rotate it while recording.

### Notifications appear in the video

Turn on Do Not Disturb before you record. On Android, swipe down and tap the Do Not Disturb tile. On iPhone, open Control Centre and tap the Focus button, then choose Do Not Disturb. Remember to turn it off when you are done, or you may miss calls and messages.

## Ideas for Using Screen Recording in Your Business

Screen recording is not just a fun phone trick — it is a practical business tool. If you run a small business in Nigeria, here are ways it earns its keep.

Show a customer how to do something instead of typing it out. If a customer asks how to order from your website, how to pay, or how to track a delivery, record your screen as you do it once, and send that video to every customer who asks. You save time and the customer understands faster.

Create short tutorials for your staff. Record how to update prices on your website, how to reply to a customer on WhatsApp Business, or how to fill in a delivery form. New staff can watch the video instead of you explaining the same thing five times.

Record video calls and online meetings so you can go back and check what was agreed. Just remember to tell the other person you are recording — it is polite, and in a business setting it avoids misunderstandings later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is screen recording free on Android and iPhone?

Yes. Both Android and iPhone have a screen recorder built into the phone, so there is nothing to buy and no app to install. If your Android phone is very old and has no built-in recorder, there are free recorder apps on the Google Play Store, but check their reviews before installing.

### How do I screen record with sound on iPhone?

Open Control Centre and press and hold the screen recording button, then tap the microphone icon to turn it on, then tap "Start Recording". A single quick tap records without your voice, which is why many iPhone recordings come out silent. The phone still captures the sound from videos playing on the phone.

### How do I screen record with sound on Android?

Tap the Screen record tile in your quick settings, and when the options appear, choose "Media sounds" to capture app audio, or "Media sounds and mic" to capture app audio plus your voice. Choose "No sound" only if the audio does not matter.

### Where are screen recordings saved?

On Android, recordings are saved in your Gallery or Photos app, usually in a folder called "Screen recordings" or "Movies". On iPhone, they are saved in the Photos app, in your main library and in the "Screen Recordings" album. From there you can share, trim or delete them like any other video.

### Why is my screen recording black when I record Netflix or my bank app?

Some apps block screen recording to protect copyrighted content or sensitive information. Streaming apps like Netflix and most banking apps will show a black screen in the recording even though everything looks normal while you watch. This is deliberate and there is no built-in way around it.

### How long can I screen record for?

There is no fixed time limit on either Android or iPhone. You can record for as long as you have free storage space on your phone. Long recordings create very large files, so check your storage before recording something long, and delete recordings you no longer need.

### Can I record a video call?

Yes, you can record a video call on both Android and iPhone using the built-in recorder. Tell the other person you are recording before you start.
