When someone searches for a product or service you offer, does your website appear in the results? If not, you are missing potential customers.

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the practice of improving your website to rank higher in search engine results. Higher rankings mean more visibility, more traffic, and more customers.

Here are the SEO basics every Nigerian business needs to know.

## How Search Engines Work

Search engines like Google use automated programs called crawlers to discover web pages. These crawlers follow links from known pages to new pages. They analyze the content of each page and index it in a massive database.

When someone performs a search, Google's algorithm searches its index for the most relevant results. The algorithm considers hundreds of factors including keyword relevance, content quality, website authority, and user experience.

## Keyword Research

Keywords are the words and phrases people type into search engines. Your goal is to rank for keywords that potential customers use when searching for your products or services.

Start by brainstorming topics related to your business. For each topic, list the phrases customers might search for. Use keyword research tools like Google Keyword Planner to find search volume and competition data. Look for keywords with decent search volume and low to medium competition.

Long-tail keywords are longer, more specific phrases. They have lower search volume but higher conversion rates because searchers know exactly what they want.

## On-Page SEO

On-page SEO optimizes individual web pages to rank higher. Each page should target one primary keyword. Include your keyword in the page title, URL, headings, and naturally throughout the content.

Write compelling meta descriptions that appear below your title in search results. Meta descriptions do not directly affect rankings but influence whether people click your result.

Use heading tags (H1, H2, H3) to structure your content. The H1 tag should contain your primary keyword. H2 and H3 tags organize subtopics.

Optimize images with descriptive file names and alt text. Alt text helps search engines understand image content and improves accessibility.

## Technical SEO

Technical SEO ensures search engines can find, crawl, and index your website. A sitemap.xml file lists all your pages and helps crawlers discover them. Submit your sitemap to Google Search Console.

Page speed affects both rankings and user experience. Slow websites rank lower and lose visitors. Compress images, use caching, and choose fast hosting. Google's PageSpeed Insights tool shows your site speed and recommendations.

Mobile-friendliness is essential because Google primarily uses the mobile version of websites for ranking. Ensure your site works well on phones and tablets.

Secure your site with HTTPS. Google considers HTTPS a ranking signal. It also builds trust with visitors.

## Link Building

Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. Google views backlinks as votes of confidence. More quality backlinks generally lead to higher rankings.

Create content that other websites want to link to — guides, research, infographics, and original data. Guest post on reputable websites in your industry. Build relationships with other businesses and websites.

Quality matters more than quantity. One link from a reputable industry website is worth more than dozens of links from low-quality directories.

## Local SEO

Local SEO helps your business appear in local search results. Create and optimize your Google Business Profile. Ensure your name, address, and phone number are consistent across all online directories. Encourage customers to leave Google reviews.
