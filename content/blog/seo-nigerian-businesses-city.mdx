## Why Local SEO Matters More in 2026

Google's algorithm has shifted dramatically toward local intent. When a user searches for "fashion boutique in Lagos" or "car wash in Lekki," Google prioritises local businesses in the search results through two main features:

1. **The Local Pack** â€” The map with three business listings that appears at the top of search results
2. **Local organic results** â€” Websites optimised for local keywords that rank below the Local Pack

Businesses that appear in these positions capture the vast majority of clicks. If you are not optimised for local search, your competitors are taking those customers.

## Step 1: Claim and Optimise Your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the single most important local SEO tool. It is free, and it determines whether you appear in the Local Pack.

### Set Up Your Profile

Go to google.com/business and claim your business. You will need to verify your business address â€” Google sends a postcard with a verification code to your physical address.

### Optimise Every Field

Most Nigerian businesses leave their Google Business Profile half-empty. Fill in everything:

- **Business name** exactly as it appears in real life (do not stuff keywords here)
- **Category** â€” Choose the most specific category that fits. A "Lagos restaurant" should select "Nigerian restaurant" not just "restaurant"
- **Address** â€” Your physical business address. If you serve customers at their locations, you can set a service area instead
- **Phone number** â€” A local Lagos number (070, 080, 081, 090 prefixes). Google uses this to confirm your location
- **Website** â€” Link to your website
- **Hours** â€” Accurate opening hours, including public holiday adjustments
- **Services** â€” List specific services. A beauty salon should list "bridal makeup," "hair styling," "mani-pedi," etc.
- **Attributes** â€” Mark features like "free Wi-Fi," "outdoor seating," "wheelchair accessible"
- **Photos** â€” Upload at least 10-15 high-quality photos. Businesses with photos get 42% more requests for directions and 35% more click-throughs to their website
- **Posts** â€” Google Business Profile allows you to publish updates, offers, and events. Post at least once a week

### Get Reviews

Reviews are the most influential ranking factor for local SEO. Google prioritises businesses with more positive reviews. Here is how to manage them:

- **Ask every satisfied customer** for a review. The best time is right after a positive interaction â€” a completed service, a purchase, a compliment
- **Make it easy** â€” Send a direct link to your review page. Use a tool like one.click.google to create a short link
- **Respond to every review** â€” Thank positive reviewers and address negative ones professionally. Google sees responsiveness as a quality signal
- **Never buy fake reviews** â€” Google's algorithm detects and penalises fake reviews. It is not worth the risk

### Common Mistakes Nigerian Businesses Make

- **Using a P.O. Box** â€” Google requires a physical street address
- **Choosing the wrong category** â€” Being too generic hurts your ranking for specific searches
- **Ignoring Q&A** â€” Customers can ask questions on your profile. Answer them promptly and thoroughly
- **Changing your business name** to include keywords (e.g., "Best Restaurant in Lekki Lagos") â€” This violates Google's guidelines and can get your profile suspended
- **Using a virtual office address** without staff present during business hours â€” Google may verify by calling or visiting

## Step 2: Optimise Your Website for Local Search

Your website needs to signal to Google that you serve a specific location.

### Location Pages

If you serve multiple areas, create separate pages for each. A cleaning service that covers Lekki, VI, and Ikoyi needs three distinct pages:

- "Professional Cleaning Services in Lekki"
- "Professional Cleaning Services in Victoria Island"
- "Professional Cleaning Services in Ikoyi"

Each page should have unique content (not just the same paragraph with different location names), local photos, and local testimonials.

### City and Neighbourhood Keywords

Include your location in key places on your website:

- Title tags: "Makeup Artist in Lekki | Lagos Bridal Makeup"
- Meta descriptions: "Book the best makeup artist in Lekki, Lagos. Specialising in bridal, party, and event makeup. Free consultation available."
- Headings: "Why Choose Our Lekki-Based Makeup Services"
- Body content: Natural mentions of your location throughout
- Image alt text: "bridal-makeup-lekki-lagos.jpg"

### NAP Consistency

NAP stands for Name, Address, Phone number. Your NAP must be identical across your entire website and every directory where your business is listed. If your address is "10 Admiralty Way, Lekki Phase 1" on your website but "10 Admiralty Way, Lekki" on Google, inconsistency hurts your ranking.

### Local Schema Markup

Schema markup is code that helps Google understand your business details. For local businesses, the LocalBusiness schema is essential. It tells Google your business name, address, phone number, opening hours, and more. Most website builders and agencies (including Joetech) include this in the build.

## Step 3: Build Local Citations

A citation is any mention of your business on the internet â€” directories, social media, review sites, news articles. Google uses citations to verify that your business is real and established.

### Priority Nigerian Directories

List your business on these platforms:

- Google Business Profile (essential)
- Bing Places for Business (free, Bing has significant market share in Nigeria)
- VConnect
- BusinessDay Business Directory
- Finelib.com
- Hotfm.ng
- Local government websites (many local councils list businesses in their area)
- Industry-specific directories (e.g., wedding directories for event planners)

### Consistency Is Critical

Your NAP must be identical on every directory. Use a spreadsheet to track where you are listed and what information appears. Even a small discrepancy â€” "Lagos" vs "Lagos State" â€” can weaken your local SEO signals.

## Step 4: Create Locally Relevant Content

Content is how you capture the other half of local search â€” the organic results below the Local Pack. To rank for organic local searches, you need content that is useful, relevant, and locally focused.

### Content Ideas for Nigerian Businesses

- "Best [Service] in [Neighbourhood]" â€” A guide comparing options in your area, mentioning your business naturally
- "How to Choose a [Service Provider] in Lagos" â€” Educational content that local customers will search for
- "[City] [Industry] Guide" â€” A comprehensive resource about your industry in your city
- "Behind the Scenes at [Your Business]" â€” Content that builds trust and includes local references
- "Events in Lagos This Month" â€” If relevant to your industry, round up local events your customers might attend

### Local Link Building

Links from other local websites signal to Google that you are part of the local business community. Ways to earn local links:

- Sponsor local events or charities (they often list sponsors on their website)
- Partner with complementary businesses for cross-promotion (e.g., a photographer and a makeup artist linking to each other)
- Get featured in local news or blogs
- Join your local business association and get listed on their website

## Step 5: Measure and Improve

Local SEO is not a one-time setup. It requires ongoing attention.

### What to Track

- **Google Business Profile insights** â€” How many people found you through search vs maps, what queries they used, how many clicked through to your website or called
- **Google Search Console** â€” Your website's organic search performance, including average position for your target keywords

- **Website traffic** â€” Use Google Analytics to measure traffic from local searches and track conversions (calls, form submissions, bookings)

### How Often to Review

- Weekly: Respond to reviews, post on Google Business Profile, check for new Q&A
- Monthly: Review analytics, check rankings for top keywords, update any outdated information
- Quarterly: Audit your directory listings for consistency, publish a new piece of locally-focused content
