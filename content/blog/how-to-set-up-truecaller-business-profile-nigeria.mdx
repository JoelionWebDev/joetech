## The Problem: Nigerians Do Not Answer Unknown Numbers

Think about the last time an unknown number called you. Did you answer it, or did you let it ring? If you are like most Nigerians, you let it ring. Spam calls, fake loan offers, and fraud attempts have trained everyone to be suspicious of numbers they do not recognise. A missed call from a genuine business looks exactly like a missed call from a scammer.

This is a serious problem for small businesses in Nigeria. You call a customer to confirm a delivery, and they do not pick up. You call a new lead who asked for a quote, and they ignore you. You call to follow up on an unpaid invoice, and the call goes unanswered. Every missed call is a lost sale, a delayed delivery, or a customer who quietly buys from someone else.

Truecaller Business Profile solves this problem. When you call from a number with a business profile, the person you are calling sees your business name, your logo, and a verification badge on their screen. Instead of "Unknown Number", they see "Joetech — Web Design" or "De Choice Supermarket". That single difference can turn an ignored call into an answered one.

This guide explains what a Truecaller Business Profile is, how to set one up step by step, how to get verified, and how to use it properly so your calls build trust instead of suspicion.

## What Is a Truecaller Business Profile?

Truecaller is a caller identification app used by millions of people in Nigeria. When someone calls, Truecaller checks the number against its database and shows the caller's name on the recipient's screen, even if the number is not saved in their contacts.

A Truecaller Business Profile is the business version of this. It lets a business control exactly what customers see when the business calls them. The profile can show your business name, your logo, your business category, your address, your website, your business hours, and a verified badge that proves the number genuinely belongs to your business.

There are two levels. The free business profile lets you set your business name, logo and basic details so customers recognise you. The paid Truecaller for Business service adds a green verified badge, a call reason that tells the customer why you are calling before they answer, and tools for managing several business numbers and seeing how many of your calls are answered.

For a small business, even the free profile is a big improvement over an unidentified number. The verified badge and call reason are worth paying for once your business makes many outbound calls a day, such as delivery confirmations, appointment reminders or sales follow-ups.

## Before You Start: What You Will Need

Setting up a business profile is straightforward, but it goes faster if you prepare everything first. Gather the following:

First, a dedicated business phone number. Do not use your personal number for business calls. A separate number — ideally the same one on your WhatsApp Business account, your Google Business Profile and your flyers — keeps your business identity consistent. Customers who see the same number everywhere will recognise it faster. If you do not have a separate number yet, get a new SIM for the business. It is a small cost that pays for itself in professionalism.

Second, your business name, exactly as you want customers to see it. Use the name customers know you by. If your shop is called "Ada's Kitchen" but your registered name is something different, use "Ada's Kitchen" — recognition matters more than formality here.

Third, a clear logo. Truecaller displays your logo next to your name when you call, so it should be simple, readable at a small size, and clearly yours. A busy logo with tiny text will be unreadable on a phone screen. Use the same logo you use on your shop sign and social media profiles so customers make the connection instantly.

Fourth, your business details: your category (such as restaurant, fashion store, or logistics company), your address, your website or Instagram page, and your business hours. Having these ready means you can complete the profile in one sitting.

Finally, a business document for verification. Truecaller typically asks for proof that the business exists, such as a CAC registration document, a business name certificate, or a utility bill in the business name. If your business is not yet registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission, you can still set up a basic profile, but verification will be harder. This is one more good reason to register your business name — it unlocks trust signals like this one.

## Step 1: Install Truecaller and Set Up the Business Number

Start by installing the Truecaller app on the phone that carries your business number. The app is available for Android and iPhone. During setup, Truecaller will ask for permission to access your contacts and to manage calls. Grant the permissions it needs to identify callers, or the app cannot do its job.

Register the app with your business number. Truecaller will send a verification code by SMS or call to confirm that you control the number. Enter the code, and the number is now linked to Truecaller.

If your business number is also your WhatsApp Business number, keep Truecaller on the same phone so the caller ID works consistently. If you use a dual-SIM phone, make sure Truecaller is set up on the business SIM, not your personal one.

Once the app is running, search for your own business number in Truecaller to see what customers currently see. If the number is new, it may show nothing, or it may show a wrong name from a previous owner of the number. That is exactly what the business profile will fix.

## Step 2: Create Your Business Profile

There are two ways to create a business profile. The first is inside the Truecaller app itself. Open the app, go to your profile settings, and look for the option to create or upgrade to a business profile. The app will guide you through entering your business name, category, address and logo.

The second way is through Truecaller's business website, which is the route for the paid Truecaller for Business service. There, you create a business account, add your number or numbers, and complete the verification process. The website route gives you more control, including the ability to manage multiple numbers, set call reasons, and view analytics on your calls.

Whichever route you use, fill in every field carefully. Your business name should be spelled exactly right — a misspelling on a caller ID looks careless and makes customers suspicious. Your category should match what you actually do. A fashion business listed as a restaurant confuses people. Your address should be your real shop or office address in Lekki, Ikeja, Abuja or wherever you operate. Your business hours should be accurate so customers know when to expect your calls.

Upload your logo in good quality. Truecaller will display it as a small circle, so check that it still looks clear when it is small. If your logo has text, make sure the text is large enough to read. If it is not, use a simplified version of your logo for Truecaller.

## Step 3: Get Verified

Verification is what separates a business profile from an ordinary Truecaller listing. A verified business shows a badge that tells customers this number has been confirmed as belonging to a real business. That badge is the trust signal that makes people answer.

To get verified, Truecaller will ask you to prove two things: that the business exists, and that you are authorised to speak for it. The documents you may need include your CAC certificate or business name registration, a government-issued ID, and sometimes a utility bill or bank statement showing the business name and address.

Submit clear photos or scans of these documents. Blurry images cause delays. Make sure the business name on the documents matches the name on your profile. If your CAC certificate says "Joetech 360 Ventures" but your profile says "Joetech", Truecaller may ask you to explain the difference or to align the names. Use your registered name as the foundation, and add your trading name where the profile allows it.

Verification usually takes a few working days. While you wait, your basic profile may already be visible to callers, but the badge appears only after approval. If verification is rejected, read the reason carefully, fix the problem, and resubmit. Common reasons for rejection are mismatched names, unreadable documents, or using a personal number instead of a business number.

If you subscribe to the paid Truecaller for Business service, verification is part of the onboarding, and the Truecaller team will guide you through it. For small businesses that make a lot of calls, the paid service is worth considering because of the call reason feature, which we cover below.

## Step 4: Set a Call Reason

The call reason is one of the most powerful features of Truecaller for Business, and it is the reason many businesses pay for the service. A call reason is a short message that appears on the customer's screen before they answer, explaining why you are calling. For example: "Your order is out for delivery", "Confirming your appointment for tomorrow", or "Following up on your quote request".

Think about how this changes the customer's decision. An unknown number calling at 2pm is suspicious. The same number calling with the message "Your order is out for delivery" is expected and welcome. The customer answers, the delivery is confirmed, and the rider does not waste time at a gate with nobody answering the phone.

Write call reasons that are short, specific and honest. "Delivery update" is good. "Your package from Ada's Kitchen is on its way" is even better. Never write a misleading call reason to trick someone into answering. If the customer answers expecting a delivery and gets a sales pitch, they will never answer your calls again — and they may report your number as spam.

Set up different call reasons for different situations if the paid service allows it. A delivery business might have one reason for dispatch, one for delivery confirmation, and one for feedback. A clinic might have one for appointment reminders and one for test results. Match the reason to the call, every time.

## Step 5: Use the Profile Properly Every Day

A business profile only builds trust if the way you call matches what the profile promises. A verified badge on a rude or pushy call destroys trust faster than no badge at all.

First, call at reasonable hours. Calling a customer at 6am or 10pm without an emergency reason is intrusive, verified badge or not. Business hours are business hours. If your business operates from 8am to 6pm, keep your calls inside those hours, except for deliveries the customer is actively expecting.

Second, identify yourself immediately. "Good afternoon, this is Chidi from Ada's Kitchen, calling about your order" takes five seconds and removes all doubt. Do not assume the customer read your caller ID. Say who you are and why you are calling, right away.

Third, keep calls short and purposeful. Customers are busy. State your reason, get the information you need, confirm the next step, and end the call politely. A two-minute call that solves the problem is better than a ten-minute call that wanders.

Fourth, never use your verified business number for spam. Do not buy lists of random numbers and call them all with sales pitches. Truecaller allows users to report spam, and a number with many spam reports can lose its good standing. Use the number to serve your real customers, and use proper marketing channels — like WhatsApp broadcasts to people who opted in, or Instagram adverts — for finding new ones.

Fifth, train your staff who make calls. If three people in your business call customers from the same number, they should all introduce themselves the same way and follow the same standards. A customer who gets a polite call on Monday and a rude one on Wednesday will blame the business, not the individual.

## Common Problems and How to Fix Them

The most common problem is a wrong name showing on your number. This usually happens when a number was previously owned by someone else, or when Truecaller picked up your name from another user's contacts. Creating a business profile and getting verified usually corrects this, because the verified profile takes priority over crowd-sourced names. If the wrong name persists after verification, contact Truecaller support through the app or the business website.

Another common problem is customers still not answering. A business profile helps, but it does not guarantee answers. If your answer rate is still low, check your call timing, check that your call reason is clear, and check that your number is not being reported as spam. If you have been calling people who never asked to be called, stop. Focus on customers who expect your call.

Some business owners worry that Truecaller shows their personal name instead of the business name. This happens when the personal profile on the number was set up first. Creating a proper business profile and completing verification fixes this. Make sure no staff member has set up a personal Truecaller profile on the business number, because that can override the business listing.

Another issue is the logo not showing. This is usually a file problem — the image may be too small, in the wrong format, or too detailed to display at small size. Upload a clean, square logo image, and check it on a real phone after it goes live. Ask a friend to call you and confirm what they see on their screen.

## Measuring Whether It Works

A business profile is an investment, so measure whether it is paying off. The simplest measure is your answer rate: out of every ten calls you make, how many are answered now, compared to before the profile? If you were getting three answers out of ten before and now get six, the profile is working.

If you use the paid service, Truecaller provides analytics showing how many calls were answered, how many went to voicemail, and how customers interacted with your call reasons. Review these numbers monthly. If a particular call reason gets a low answer rate, rewrite it. If calls at a certain time of day are rarely answered, change the timing.

Also listen to what customers say. If customers start saying "I saw it was your shop calling, so I picked up", the profile is doing its job. If they still say "I didn't know who it was", something is wrong with the setup and you should check what actually appears on their screen.

## Final Thoughts

In a country where spam and fraud calls are a daily experience, trust is a competitive advantage. A Truecaller Business Profile is one of the cheapest ways for a Nigerian small business to buy that trust. It costs little or nothing to set up, it takes less than an hour, and it changes what every customer sees when you call.

Set up a dedicated business number. Create the profile with your real name, logo and details. Get verified. Use call reasons honestly. And call your customers the way you would want to be called — politely, briefly, and at a reasonable hour.

The next time you call a customer, they will see your business name and your badge, and they will answer. That answered call is the difference between a delivered order and a missed one, between a confirmed appointment and an empty chair, and between a sale and a lost customer.
