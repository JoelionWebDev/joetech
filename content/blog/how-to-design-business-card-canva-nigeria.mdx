A business card is small, but in Nigeria it still carries weight. At a trade fair in Lagos, a church programme, a wedding, or a meeting in an office in Abuja, a neat card can be the difference between a new customer remembering you and forgetting you by the time they reach their car. People may lose a flyer, but they often keep a good card in their wallet or bag.

Many small business owners think they need a graphic designer and a large budget to get a proper card. You do not. Canva is a free design app that works on your phone and on a computer. It has ready-made business card templates that you can change to fit your business in under an hour. You do not need design experience, and you do not need to pay for the expensive version to get a good result.

This guide shows you how to design a business card with Canva in Nigeria, step by step. You will learn what to put on the card, how to choose colours and fonts that suit your business, how to add your logo and photo, and how to print the card affordably. The examples cover a tailor in Surulere, a caterer in Lekki, a real estate agent in Abuja, and a phone repair shop in Ikeja. The same steps work for almost any small business.

## What Makes a Good Business Card in Nigeria

A good business card is clear, honest, and easy to read. A customer should understand what you do within three seconds of looking at it. If they have to squint, turn the card around, or guess your service, the card has failed. Keep the design simple and let the important details stand out.

Start with your business name in large, bold letters. That is the most important text on the card. Under it, write one short line that explains what you do, such as "Ladies fashion and ready-to-wear" or "Home catering for weddings and parties." Do not write a long paragraph about your business. One clear line is enough, and it leaves space for your contact details.

Include only the contact details customers will actually use. Your phone number and WhatsApp number are the most important, because most Nigerian customers will call or message you first. Add your location, such as your shop address or area, and your Instagram handle or website if you have one. Do not crowd the card with five phone numbers, three email addresses, and every social media account you own. Choose the best one or two ways to reach you.

Your card should look like your business. A fashion brand can use bright colours and a stylish font. A law firm or a real estate agency should use calm colours and a plain, professional font. A food business can use warm colours that suggest fresh food. The card is often the first impression a customer gets, so make it match the quality of the work you do.

Finally, use good paper when you print. A thin, flimsy card feels cheap, even if the design is beautiful. A slightly thicker card feels solid and professional in the hand. You do not need the most expensive paper in the market, but avoid the thinnest option. Ask your printer to show you samples before you pay for a full batch.

## Setting Up Canva and Choosing a Template

Canva is free to download from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store, and it also works in a web browser on a computer. Create a free account with your email address or your Google account. You do not need to pay for Canva Pro to design a good business card. The free version has enough templates, fonts, and colours for most small businesses.

Once you are signed in, search for "business card" in Canva. You will see many templates in the standard size, which is about 3.5 inches by 2 inches. Choose a template that is close to what you want, even if the colours and words are wrong. It is easier to change a template than to start from a blank page, especially if this is your first design.

Pick a template with plenty of empty space. A crowded template will be hard to read after you add your own details. A clean template with one strong colour and clear text is a safer choice than a busy template with many pictures and patterns. Remember, the card must still be readable when printed small, so simplicity matters more than decoration.

Before you change anything, save a copy of the template design. That way, if you make a mistake, you can go back to the original. Canva saves your work automatically when you are online, but it is still wise to duplicate the design before major changes. Work on a copy, keep the original safe, and experiment freely without fear of losing your work.

If your phone screen feels too small, try designing on a computer at a cyber cafe or on a friend's laptop. A bigger screen makes it easier to line up text and check spacing. You can start the design on your phone and finish it on a computer, because Canva saves your work to your account and opens it on any device.

## Adding Your Business Name, Logo, and Details

Your business name goes first. Type it exactly as customers know it, with the correct spelling. Check the spelling twice, because a spelling mistake on a printed card is expensive to fix. If your business has a tagline, keep it short and place it under the name in smaller text. A tagline should explain your service, not repeat your name.

Add your logo if you have one. Upload the logo file to Canva and place it in a corner or at the top of the card. Make sure the logo is clear and not stretched or squashed. If your logo has a white background and your card is coloured, the white box will show and look untidy. Ask the person who made your logo for a version with a transparent background, or place the logo on a white area of the card.

Add your contact details one at a time and check the spacing after each one. Your phone number should be large enough to read easily. Write it in the format Nigerians use, starting with 080, 081, or 090, or with the country code if you serve customers outside Nigeria. Add a small WhatsApp icon or the word "WhatsApp" next to the number if that is the best way to reach you.

Add your address in short form. You do not need your full street address if your shop is in a well-known area, but include enough detail for a customer to find you, such as "Shop 12, Adeniran Ogunsanya Mall, Surulere." If you work from home and do not want to share your home address, write only your area, such as "Lekki, Lagos," and your delivery areas.

Check every detail before you finish. Call the phone number on the card to be sure it is correct. Visit the Instagram handle or website address to be sure it opens. Ask a friend to read the card and tell you what they understand. A fresh pair of eyes often catches a mistake you have missed after staring at the design for an hour.

## Choosing Colours and Fonts That Look Professional

Colours and fonts decide whether your card looks professional or amateur. Choose one main colour, one supporting colour, and white or black for text. That is enough. A card with five bright colours looks noisy and hard to read. Your main colour should match your business brand, such as the colour of your shop sign or your Instagram page.

Use dark text on a light background, or light text on a dark background. This contrast makes the text easy to read. Avoid light grey text on a white card, or yellow text on a bright background. Those combinations look fine on a phone screen but are hard to read in print, especially under the bright sun in a Lagos market.

Choose one font for your business name and a second, simpler font for the details. Canva has many fonts, but plain, clean fonts work best for business cards. Avoid fancy script fonts for phone numbers and addresses, because they are hard to read at small sizes. If you use a stylish font for your name, keep the contact details in a simple font so customers can read them quickly.

Keep the font sizes sensible. Your business name can be the largest text on the card. Your phone number should be the next largest, because that is what customers need most. Your address and social media handles can be smaller, but they must still be readable without a magnifying glass. Print a test copy on plain paper and read it at arm's length. If you cannot read it easily, increase the size.

Be consistent with your other branding. If your shop sign is blue and white, your card should be blue and white too. If your Instagram page uses gold and black, use those colours on your card. Consistency helps customers recognise your business anywhere, and it makes a small business look organised and trustworthy.

## Designing the Back of Your Card

Many beginners leave the back of the card blank, but the back is valuable space. Use it wisely and it can bring you more business. The simplest good use is to put your logo large in the centre, with your tagline underneath. That looks clean and professional, and it reminds the customer of your brand every time they turn the card over.

Another good use is to list your main services. A caterer can list "Weddings, birthdays, corporate events" on the back. A tailor can list "Native wear, suits, alterations." A phone repair shop can list "Screen replacement, battery, software." Keep the list short, with three to five items at most. A long list will look crowded and customers will not read it.

You can also use the back for a special offer or a reminder. A salon can print "Bring this card for a discount on your next visit." A food vendor can print a simple menu of best sellers with prices. A real estate agent can print a QR code that opens their WhatsApp chat or their property listings. A QR code is easy to create in Canva and easy for customers to scan with their phones.

If you sell products online, the back of the card is a good place for your digital business card link or your Instagram handle in large text. You can create a simple digital card with your photo, services, and contact buttons using Joetech's free digital business card tool at /tools/digital-business-card, then print its link or QR code on the back of your paper card so customers can save your details on their phones.

Do not put too much on the back. Choose one idea: a large logo, a short service list, a QR code, or a simple offer. The back should support the front, not compete with it. Leave some empty space so the card feels calm and easy to read.

## Printing Your Card Affordably in Nigeria

Once your design is ready, download it from Canva as a PDF for printing. Choose the PDF Print option, which gives a high-quality file that printers can use. Do not download a small image file for printing, because it will look blurry. Save the file with your business name so you can find it again, and keep a copy on your phone and on Google Drive.

Take the file to a local printer in your area. Printing shops in Ikeja, Surulere, Yaba, and Wuse in Abuja print business cards every day and know the standard sizes. Ask for a price for a pack of one hundred cards first, and ask what paper options they have. Compare two or three printers before you choose, because prices can differ a lot for the same quality.

Ask the printer to show you a sample before printing the full batch. Check the colours, the spelling, and the alignment. Colours on a phone screen can look different on paper, so a sample helps you catch problems early. If the colours look wrong, adjust the design in Canva and ask for another sample. It is better to spend a little extra time now than to pay for a hundred cards with a mistake.

Order a small batch first if this is your first card. One hundred cards is a good starting amount for most small businesses. That is enough to share at meetings, markets, and events for a few weeks, without wasting money if you later change your phone number or address. When your details change, update the design in Canva and print a new batch.

Keep your cards in a clean card holder or a small box, not loose in a pocket where they bend and get dirty. A bent, dirty card makes a poor impression when you hand it to a potential customer. Carry a few cards with you every day, because you never know when you will meet someone who needs your service. A card given at the right moment can lead to a customer you would never have found online.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Canva free for designing business cards?

Yes, the free version of Canva is enough for a good business card. You can use free templates, fonts, and colours without paying. Canva Pro adds more templates and features, but you do not need it to start. Design your first card with the free version, and only consider paying when your business grows and you want more options.

### What size should a business card be?

The standard size is about 3.5 inches by 2 inches, which is the size Canva uses for its business card templates. This size fits easily in a wallet or a card holder. Your printer in Nigeria will know this size. Do not design a card that is much larger or smaller, because it will be awkward to carry and store.

### Can I design a business card on my phone?

Yes, the Canva app works well on a phone. You can choose a template, change the text, add your logo, and download the design from your phone. A bigger screen on a computer makes lining up text easier, but a phone is enough. Start on your phone, and check the final design on a bigger screen if you can before printing.

### How many cards should I print first?

One hundred cards is a good first batch for most small businesses. It is affordable, and it is enough to share for a few weeks. If you change your number, address, or prices often, print small batches so you do not waste cards. When your details are stable and you give cards out often, you can order larger batches at a lower cost per card.

### Should I put my photo on my business card?

It depends on your business. A real estate agent, a consultant, or a personal stylist may benefit from a clear, professional photo, because customers remember faces. A shop or a food business usually does not need a personal photo. If you use a photo, make sure it is sharp, well lit, and recent. A blurry or outdated photo will do more harm than good.
