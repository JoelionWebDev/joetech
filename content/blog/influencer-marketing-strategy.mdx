People trust recommendations from people they follow more than they trust advertising. Influencer marketing leverages this trust by partnering with content creators who have established audiences.

When an influencer recommends your product, their followers pay attention. The recommendation feels personal and authentic, not like an advertisement.

Here is how to build an influencer marketing strategy for your Nigerian business.

## Types of Influencers

Nano-influencers have one thousand to ten thousand followers. They have the highest engagement rates and most personal connections with their audience. They are affordable and effective for local businesses. A nano-influencer in Lagos can drive significant traffic to a local business.

Micro-influencers have ten thousand to one hundred thousand followers. They balance reach and engagement. Micro-influencers are considered experts in their niche. They are the most popular choice for brand partnerships.

Macro-influencers have one hundred thousand to one million followers. They offer broad reach but lower engagement rates. They are suitable for brand awareness campaigns.

Mega-influencers have over one million followers. They are celebrities or major content creators. Partnerships are expensive and suitable for large brands.

## Finding the Right Influencers

Relevance matters more than follower count. An influencer with ten thousand followers in your niche is more valuable than one with one hundred thousand followers in an unrelated niche.

Engagement rate is more important than follower count. An influencer with twenty thousand followers and ten percent engagement reaches more people effectively than one with one hundred thousand followers and one percent engagement.

Authenticity matters. Influencers who promote too many products lose credibility with their audience. Look for influencers who are selective about partnerships.

Audience alignment ensures the influencer's followers match your target customer. Check their audience demographics if available. Review their comments to see if followers match your target market.

## Approaching Influencers

Personalize your outreach. Mention specific content you enjoy from them. Explain why you think a partnership would be valuable for their audience.

Be clear about expectations. Specify deliverables, timeline, and compensation. Provide creative freedom — influencers know their audience best.

Compensation varies. Payment can be monetary, free products, affiliate commissions, or a combination. Nano and micro-influencers may accept free products. Larger influencers typically require payment.

## Campaign Types

Product reviews feature the influencer trying and reviewing your product. This builds trust because the influencer's honest opinion is shared.

Sponsored posts are paid content where the influencer promotes your product. The content should feel natural within the influencer's usual style.

Giveaways partner with influencers to give away your products. This drives engagement and grows both your and the influencer's audience.

Affiliate marketing provides influencers with unique discount codes or tracking links. They earn commission on sales they generate. This aligns their incentives with yours.

## Measuring Success

Track reach, engagement, website traffic, conversions, and return on investment. Use unique discount codes or tracking links to attribute sales to specific influencers.

Monitor brand sentiment in comments and mentions. Positive sentiment indicates a successful partnership.
