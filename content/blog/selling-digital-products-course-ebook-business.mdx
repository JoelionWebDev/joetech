You have skills that other people want to learn. You know how to build a React app, deploy a website, integrate a payment gateway, or start freelancing. That knowledge has value.

The most scalable way to monetize your expertise is by packaging it into a digital product — a course, ebook, or template — and selling it online. Unlike freelancing, where you trade time for money, a digital product sells while you sleep.

Nigerian tech professionals are increasingly building digital product businesses. A course on Selar priced at ₦15,000 with 200 buyers generates ₦3,000,000. An ebook on Gumroad at $10 with 500 buyers generates $5,000.

This article walks through how to package your expertise into a paid course or ebook.

## What to Sell

The best digital product is the one you are uniquely qualified to create. What do you know that others are trying to learn?

Teaching a skill you use daily is the obvious choice. If you build apps with Flutter, create a Flutter course. If you have landed multiple remote jobs, write a guide on remote job hunting for Nigerian developers.

Solving a specific problem works well. Courses and ebooks that help people achieve a concrete outcome outperform general knowledge products. How to Build and Deploy a Portfolio Website is more sellable than Introduction to Web Development.

Narrow your topic. A course on React that covers every concept is overwhelming. A course on React State Management with Redux Toolkit is focused and easier to create.

## Choosing a Platform

Selar is the leading Nigerian platform for digital products. It supports Naira pricing, local payment methods, and instant bank transfers. Selar handles delivery, payment processing, and basic marketing. For products targeting a Nigerian audience, Selar is the best choice.

Gumroad is popular for international audiences. It supports USD pricing and global payments. Setting up a Gumroad account requires a US bank account or Payoneer account. Gumroad takes a small percentage of each sale.

Teachable is a full-featured course platform with built-in video hosting, quizzes, and student management. It is suitable for comprehensive courses with multiple modules. Teachable charges a monthly fee plus transaction fees.

Your own website gives you full control. Use a platform like Paystack to process payments and deliver content. This approach requires more setup but keeps all revenue.

## Creating the Product

Start with an outline. Break your topic into logical sections. Each section should cover one concept or skill. A course with five to ten modules is manageable. An ebook with ten to fifteen chapters is substantial.

Create the content. For a course, record screen capture videos with voiceover. Keep each video between five and fifteen minutes. Edit out mistakes and long pauses. For an ebook, write clearly and include screenshots and code examples where helpful.

Quality matters, but perfection is the enemy of done. Your first product does not need to be perfect. It needs to be good enough to deliver value. You can improve it based on buyer feedback.

Add supplementary materials. Checklists, templates, code repositories, and cheat sheets increase the perceived value of your product.

## Pricing

Nigerian buyers expect lower prices than international buyers. A course priced at ₦10,000 to ₦25,000 is reasonable for the Nigerian market. An ebook priced at ₦3,000 to ₦7,000 sells well.

International buyers can pay higher prices. A course at $30 to $100 or an ebook at $10 to $20 is standard on Gumroad and Teachable.

Test different price points. Start at a moderate price and adjust based on sales data. Use launch discounts to generate initial sales and reviews.

## Marketing Your Product

Your existing network is your first market. Share your product with your email list, social media followers, and professional network. Personal messages to people who know your expertise work better than broadcast announcements.

Content marketing builds demand for your product over time. Create free content — blog posts, YouTube videos, Twitter threads — that demonstrate your expertise. People who find your free content valuable are likely to buy your paid content.

Collaborate with other creators for cross-promotion. Partner with someone who has an audience that overlaps with yours. You promote each other's products.

## Conclusion

A digital product business is one of the best ways for Nigerian tech professionals to generate passive income. Your expertise is valuable. Packaging it into a course or ebook lets you earn from that expertise repeatedly.

Start with one product. Focus on a narrow topic you know well. Use a platform that matches your target audience. Promote it consistently. Improve it based on feedback. Add more products over time.
