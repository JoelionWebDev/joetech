Video dominates social media. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter all prioritize video content in their algorithms. But most business videos get ignored.

The difference between a viral video and an ignored one is strategy. Successful social media videos are designed for the platform, optimized for the feed, and created with a specific goal.

Here is how to create a social media video strategy that drives engagement and shares for your Nigerian business.

## Platform-Specific Approach

Each social platform has unique video requirements. A video that works on TikTok may not work on LinkedIn.

Instagram and TikTok favor short, vertical videos under sixty seconds. Content should be visually engaging from the first frame. Trends, challenges, and music-driven content perform well. Post daily for maximum reach.

Facebook supports longer videos but auto-plays without sound. Captions are essential. Native uploads perform better than shared YouTube links. Facebook Watch is a growing discovery platform.

LinkedIn favors professional, educational content. Longer videos (two to five minutes) are acceptable. Thought leadership, industry insights, and behind-the-scenes professional content performs well.

YouTube is a search platform. Videos should be optimized for search with titles, descriptions, and tags. Longer content (five to fifteen minutes) is expected. YouTube Shorts is the platform's answer to TikTok.

Twitter videos should be under forty-five seconds. They autoplay in the feed. News, commentary, and timely content perform well.

## The Hook

The first three seconds determine whether a viewer continues watching or scrolls past. Your opening must grab attention immediately.

Start with a provocative statement. The reason most Nigerian businesses fail at social media is that they do not understand their audience. This creates curiosity and makes viewers want to hear the explanation.

Start with a question. Are you wasting money on social media ads? Viewers who have this question will keep watching for the answer.

Start with a surprising visual. An unexpected image, fast-paced edit, or striking graphic stops the scroll. Visual hooks are particularly effective on Instagram and TikTok.

## Content Pillars

Educational content teaches your audience something valuable. How-to videos, tips and tricks, industry insights, and explanations of complex topics position you as an expert.

Entertainment content makes people laugh or feel good. Humor, heartwarming stories, and creative content builds brand affinity. Entertaining content is shared more than any other type.

Inspirational content motivates your audience. Success stories, customer transformations, and behind-the-scenes perseverance stories inspire viewers.

Promotional content directly promotes your product or service. Limit promotional content to twenty percent of your total output. Too much promotion drives viewers away.

## Format Optimization

Vertical video fills the mobile screen and captures attention. Film and edit in vertical format for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

Captions are essential because most social media videos are watched without sound. Add captions to every video. This increases watch time and accessibility.

Keep it short. Social media attention spans are measured in seconds. Cut anything that does not serve the core message. Aim for fifteen to sixty seconds for most social platforms.

## Consistency and Frequency

Post consistently. Algorithms favor channels that post regularly. Create a content calendar and stick to a schedule. Daily posting is ideal for Instagram and TikTok. Three to five times per week is sufficient for LinkedIn and Facebook.

Batch your content production. Film multiple videos in one session. This reduces production overhead and ensures consistent output.
