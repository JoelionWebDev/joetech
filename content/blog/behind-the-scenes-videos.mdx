Customers buy from people they know and trust. Behind-the-scenes videos introduce your team, show your process, and reveal the human side of your business.

In a world of polished marketing, authenticity stands out. Behind-the-scenes content is inherently authentic. It is not scripted or rehearsed. It shows real people doing real work.

Here is how behind-the-scenes videos can humanize your Nigerian business and build stronger connections with your customers.

## Why Behind-the-Scenes Content Works

Behind-the-scenes videos build trust because they are transparent. Showing how your product is made or how your team operates demonstrates confidence in your process. If you have nothing to hide, you have nothing to fear from showing the truth.

They build relationships because customers feel connected to the people behind the brand. A customer who knows the founder's name or has seen the team working feels more personally connected to the business.

They differentiate your brand because most businesses do not share behind-the-scenes content. In a sea of similar marketing messages, transparency stands out. It makes your brand memorable.

## What to Show in Behind-the-Scenes Videos

Show your workspace. Take viewers through your office, workshop, or studio. Show where the work happens. The environment reflects your company culture and values.

Show your team. Introduce team members and their roles. Let them talk about what they do and why they enjoy it. Personal stories create emotional connections with viewers.

Show your process. How is your product made? How is a service delivered? Showing the effort and craftsmanship behind your offering increases perceived value. Customers appreciate quality when they see what goes into it.

Show your challenges. Not everything goes perfectly. Showing how you handle challenges, mistakes, or delays demonstrates integrity. Customers respect honesty about imperfections.

Show your celebrations. Share team achievements, birthdays, and milestones. Celebratory moments show that your team enjoys working together. Happy teams create positive brand associations.

## Types of Behind-the-Scenes Videos

Day-in-the-life videos follow a team member through their typical day. This format is relatable and easy to produce. It requires minimal planning beyond choosing which team member to feature.

Process videos show how a product is made or a service is delivered. These are particularly effective for physical products, food businesses, and service providers. The transformation from raw materials to finished product is inherently interesting.

Event preparation videos show the work that goes into a launch, exhibition, or company event. The anticipation and preparation create narrative tension that keeps viewers engaged.

Q and A with the team allows customers to ask questions directly. Collect questions from social media and have team members answer them on camera. This format engages your existing audience.

## Production Approach

Behind-the-scenes videos do not need high production values. In fact, polished production can undermine the authentic feel. Use your phone, natural lighting, and minimal equipment.

The goal is authenticity, not perfection. Imperfect footage feels real. Minor audio issues or camera shake are acceptable if the content is genuine and interesting.

That said, ensure basic quality. Audio should be clear enough to understand. Lighting should be bright enough to see. Avoid distracting background noise or excessively shaky footage.

## Distribution Strategy

Share behind-the-scenes content on Instagram Stories and Reels. These platforms are designed for casual, authentic content. The temporary nature of Stories encourages more candid sharing.

Share longer behind-the-scenes content on YouTube. Process videos and day-in-the-life content perform well as YouTube videos.

Share on LinkedIn for a professional audience. Team introductions and workplace culture content resonates with the LinkedIn community.

Repurpose across platforms. A single filming session can produce content for Stories, Reels, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn. Edit for each platform's format and audience.
