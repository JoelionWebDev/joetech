# How to Make Marketing Videos with Canva in Nigeria

A short video can do what a long sales message cannot. In a few seconds, a customer in Lekki, Ikeja, Abuja or Port Harcourt can see your product, understand what it does, and decide to send you a message. That is why video has become the most powerful marketing tool for small businesses in Nigeria. You do not need a camera crew, a studio, or expensive editing software to start. With Canva, a tool many Nigerian business owners already use for flyers and logos, you can create clean, professional marketing videos from your phone or laptop.

This guide walks you through the whole process step by step. You will learn how to plan a video that sells, how to use Canva's video editor, how to add text, music, voice and branding, and how to export and post your video where your customers actually spend their time. Whether you sell fashion in Lagos, run a restaurant in Abuja, offer beauty services in Enugu, or manage a small online store that delivers nationwide, the steps here will work for you. You can start with the free version of Canva, and you do not need to pay anything to make your first videos. A simple video posted every week will bring more customers than a perfect video you never finish.

## Why Video Marketing Works for Nigerian Businesses

Nigerians love video. People scroll through WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook every day, and video stops the scroll faster than a still image. A picture of a dress tells a customer what it looks like. A fifteen-second video shows how the fabric moves, how it fits, and how happy the person wearing it looks. That extra detail builds trust, and trust is what turns a viewer into a buyer.

Video also helps you explain things quickly. If you sell a skincare product, you can show the result in seconds. If you run a catering business, you can show food being prepared and served at an event in Lekki. If you offer cleaning or home repairs, a short clip of your team at work proves you are real and reliable. Customers in Nigeria are careful about who they send money to online, so showing your real work on video is one of the fastest ways to build confidence. Social media platforms also push video to more people than ordinary posts, and a good Reel or TikTok video can reach customers in other states who have never heard of your business.

## What You Need Before You Start

Before you open Canva, gather a few things so your session goes smoothly. First, you need a Canva account. Go to canva.com or download the Canva app from the Play Store or App Store, then sign up with your email address or Google account. The free account is enough to begin. Use a business email if you have one, so your designs stay organised under your brand.

Second, collect your brand materials. This includes your business logo, your brand colours, and photos or short clips of your products. If you have product photos on your phone, move the best ones into a folder you can find easily. Take photos in natural daylight, against a clean background, and avoid blurry images. If you sell food, photograph it fresh and well presented. If you sell clothes, photograph them on a hanger or a model in good light.

## Step 1: Plan Your Video in Five Minutes

Planning saves you hours. Before you touch a template, answer five quick questions in your phone notes. First, what is the one product, service or offer in this video? Keep it to one thing. A video that tries to sell five products at once confuses people. Pick one product and focus on it.

Second, what is the main benefit for the customer? Do not just list features. Instead of saying "this cream contains shea butter," say "this cream keeps your skin soft all day, even in harmattan." Benefits answer the customer's real question: what will this do for me? Third, what will you show on screen? List three or four short scenes. For a fashion video, that might be the dress on a hanger, the dress being worn, a close-up of the fabric, and the price and WhatsApp number at the end. Keep each scene to about three to five seconds.

Fourth, what words will appear on screen? Write the exact text for each scene before you start designing. Keep it short, no more than five or six words per screen where possible. Examples include "New Arrival," "Soft and Comfortable," and "Only ₦25,000." Fifth, what is your call to action? Tell viewers exactly what to do next: "Send us a DM," "Visit our shop at Chevron Drive, Lekki," or "WhatsApp 0803 000 0000 to order." One clear instruction is better than three vague ones.

## Step 2: Choose the Right Video Format

Canva offers many video sizes, and choosing the right one matters because each platform displays video differently. For Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels, use vertical video, which is 1080 by 1920 pixels. This fills the phone screen and is the format most Nigerians watch. Search for "Instagram Reel" or "TikTok Video" in Canva to find the right size.

For WhatsApp Status, vertical video also works well. Keep Status videos under thirty seconds if possible. A vertical video made for Instagram can be reused on WhatsApp Status without any changes, which saves you time. For YouTube Shorts, use the same vertical size. For a regular YouTube video, Facebook feed post, or a video you want to place on your website, use horizontal video, which is 1920 by 1080 pixels. For a square Facebook or Instagram feed post, Canva also has a square size of 1080 by 1080 pixels.

A practical approach is to make one vertical video first, because it works on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Reels and WhatsApp Status. That is four platforms from one video. Later, when you have more time, you can make a horizontal version for YouTube or your website. Do not try to make every size at once when you are starting out. Master one format, post regularly, and expand later.

## Step 3: Pick a Template or Start from Scratch

When you open Canva and search for a video type, you will see many templates. Templates are ready-made designs with animation, text and timing already set up. They are the fastest way to make a good-looking video. Browse them and pick one that matches your product and your brand colours. A fashion business might choose a bright, stylish template. A food business might choose a warm template. A professional service might choose a clean, simple template.

Once you pick a template, change everything in it to make it yours. Replace the photos with your own product photos. Change the text to your own words. Change the colours to your brand colours. Change the music if you want a different feel. The template is only a starting point, not a finished video.

## Step 4: Add Your Photos and Video Clips

Photos and clips are the heart of your marketing video. In Canva, click "Uploads" to add your own photos and short video clips from your phone or computer. You can also use Canva's free stock photos and videos by clicking "Elements" and searching for what you need. Search for "Nigerian food," "African fashion," or "happy customer" to find useful stock content. Stock content helps when you do not have your own photos yet, but your own real photos will always feel more trustworthy to customers.

Drag each photo or clip onto the timeline at the bottom of the screen. The timeline shows your video as a series of scenes. Each scene is one photo or clip that plays for a few seconds. To change how long a scene lasts, drag its edge left or right. For marketing videos, keep each scene between three and five seconds. Short scenes keep the video moving and hold attention.

## Step 5: Add Text That Sells

Text on video does a lot of work. Many people watch videos without sound, especially on public transport or at work. Your text must tell the whole story even when the sound is off. That means your headline, price and contact details must appear as text on screen, not only in the voiceover or music.

Start with a strong opening line in the first two seconds. This is your hook, and it decides whether someone keeps watching or scrolls past. Good hooks are short and specific: "Your skin will thank you," "Lagos delivery in 24 hours," and "New Ankara styles just landed" all work because they speak directly to a need. Avoid vague openings like "Welcome to our page." Nobody stops scrolling for that.

Use large, bold fonts for your main message and stick to clean, readable fonts. Make sure the text contrasts with the background. White text on a dark photo is easy to read, and black text on a light photo also works. If your photo is busy, add a solid colour block or a shadow behind the text so it stands out. Keep your text short on each screen, one idea per screen. Instead of a long sentence, break it into short screens: "Handmade in Lagos," "Nationwide Delivery," and "From ₦35,000." Add your price and contact details near the end, and keep the final screen on for at least three seconds so people can save the number or take a screenshot.

## Step 6: Add Music, Voice and Sound

Music sets the mood of your video. A fashion video might use upbeat Afrobeats. A spa or skincare video might use calm, gentle music. A food video might use lively, cheerful music. In Canva, click "Audio" to browse free music tracks. You can search by mood, such as "happy," "calm," or "energetic." Listen to a few options and pick one that fits your brand and does not overpower your message.

Keep the music volume lower than your voice if you add a voiceover. Canva lets you adjust the volume of each audio track separately. If your video has no voice, the music can be louder, but it should never feel harsh. Remember that many viewers watch without sound, so music is a bonus, not a requirement. Your video must still work on silent.

Adding your own voice makes a video more personal and more persuasive. You can record a voiceover directly in Canva or record it on your phone and upload the audio file. Find a quiet room, hold your phone close, and speak clearly and warmly, as if you are talking to one customer. Keep it short. A fifteen-second voiceover is enough for a thirty-second video. Say what the product is, why it is good, and what the customer should do next. If you are shy about using your own voice, rely on text and music instead. That is perfectly fine, and many successful Nigerian business videos use only text and music. Stick to music in Canva's library, because it is cleared for use in Canva designs. Avoid adding popular songs from other places, because they can cause copyright problems and your video may be muted or removed.

## Step 7: Brand Your Video Like a Professional

Branding is what makes people recognise your videos instantly. When someone sees your colours, your logo and your style repeatedly, they start to remember your business. First, add your logo to every video. Place it in the same corner each time, usually the top right or bottom right, and keep it small. It should be visible but not cover your product. If you do not have a logo yet, use your business name in a consistent font and colour instead.

Second, use the same two or three brand colours in every video. If your brand colour is blue, use blue for your text backgrounds, your price tags and your final screen. Over time, your followers will spot your videos in their feed before they even read the name. Third, use the same font for your headlines in every video. Pick one bold font you like and use it for all your video titles. Use a second, simpler font for smaller details like prices and contact information. Changing fonts in every video makes your brand look scattered.

Fourth, create a simple end screen that you reuse. Your end screen should have your logo, your business name, your WhatsApp number, and your Instagram handle. Save this as a template in Canva so you can add it to every video in seconds. This end screen is your digital business card, and it reminds viewers how to reach you. Fifth, add a short brand tagline if you have one, such as "Quality you can trust" or "Made with love in Lagos." Your tagline, colours, logo and font together create a brand identity that grows stronger with every video you post.

## Step 8: Edit, Preview and Export Your Video

Before you export, watch your video from start to finish at least twice. Watch it once with sound and once without sound. Check that the text is easy to read, the photos are clear, the music fits, and the timing feels natural. Check that your phone number is correct. A wrong digit in your WhatsApp number means lost customers, so double-check it every time.

Check the spelling of every word on screen. A spelling mistake makes a business look careless, even if the product is excellent. Ask a friend or family member to watch the video and tell you honestly what they think. Fresh eyes often catch mistakes you missed. Ask them: does the video make sense, is the price clear, and would you know how to order after watching it?

When you are happy, click "Share" and then "Download," choose MP4, and pick 1080p if your connection can handle it, or 720p if your data is limited. Save the file with a clear name, keep a copy in Google Drive, and post it on WhatsApp Status, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok with a clear caption.

## Your Video Marketing Checklist

Use this checklist every time you make a video with Canva, until the process becomes second nature. First, write down the goal of the video and the one product it features. Second, choose the right size for the platform where you will post it, usually vertical for Reels, TikTok and WhatsApp Status. Third, pick a template that fits your brand or start from a blank design. Fourth, add your own clear photos or short clips, keeping each scene to three to five seconds. Fifth, add short, bold text that tells the story even without sound, including a strong opening line, the price, and your contact details. Sixth, add music that fits the mood and a voiceover if you feel comfortable. Seventh, add your logo and brand colours consistently. Eighth, preview the video with and without sound, check spelling and your phone number, and export as MP4. Ninth, post it on WhatsApp Status, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok with a clear caption. Tenth, reply to comments and messages quickly and track which videos bring in the most enquiries.

You now have everything you need to start making marketing videos with Canva in Nigeria. You do not need expensive equipment or professional training. You need a clear plan, good photos, short honest text, and the discipline to post regularly. Open Canva today, pick a template, and make your first video. It does not have to be perfect. It has to be posted. Your customers are scrolling right now, and your next video could be the one that brings in your next order.
