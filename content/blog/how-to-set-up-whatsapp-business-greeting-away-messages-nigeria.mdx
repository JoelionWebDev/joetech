If you run a small business in Nigeria, WhatsApp is probably where most of your customers find you first. A buyer in Lekki sees your dress on Instagram, taps the WhatsApp link in your bio, and asks, "Hi, is this available?" A customer in Abuja finds your number on a flyer and asks for your price list. A regular in Ikeja wants to confirm your opening hours before driving over.

What happens next decides whether that person becomes a paying customer or moves on. If you reply quickly with a warm, clear message, you build trust at once. If the chat stays silent for hours because you are in traffic on the Lekki-Epe Expressway, attending to another customer, cooking, sewing, or simply asleep, that buyer may assume you are not serious and buy elsewhere.

That is why greeting messages and away messages on WhatsApp Business matter so much. They are automatic replies that speak for you when you cannot reply yourself. A greeting message welcomes every new customer who messages you for the first time. An away message tells people you are unavailable right now, when you will be back, and what they can do in the meantime. Together, they make a one-person business in Surulere, Onitsha, Ibadan, Port Harcourt or Kano look organised and reliable, without hiring a receptionist or staying glued to your phone all day.

This guide explains how to set them up, what to write, and mistakes to avoid. You do not need any technical skill.

## What Are Greeting and Away Messages?

A greeting message is an automatic welcome sent to customers who message your business for the first time, or after fourteen days of no conversation. It introduces your business, confirms the person has reached the right place, and guides them to the next step, such as telling you what they want, asking for a price list, or viewing your catalogue.

An away message is an automatic reply sent when you are not available. You can set it to run on a schedule, for example every evening after closing time, or turn it on manually when you travel, attend a wedding, go to the market to restock, or take a day off. It tells the customer you have received their message, you are not ignoring them, and when they should expect a personal reply.

Both messages work only in the WhatsApp Business app, not in regular WhatsApp. If you are still using personal WhatsApp for your business, switching is worth it. WhatsApp Business is free, and it gives you a business profile, a catalogue, labels, quick replies, and these automatic messages. Your chats, contacts and groups carry over when you switch, so you do not lose your customers.

## Why They Matter So Much in Nigeria

Nigerian customers message businesses at all hours. Someone may see your status update at 11 pm and ask about a product. A buyer in another time zone may message at 3 am. A busy trader may only have time to chat during a lunch break. If your only reply is silence, you lose sales you never knew about.

Speed matters. Many Nigerian buyers compare two or three sellers at the same time. They send the same question — "How much is this wig?" or "Do you deliver to Enugu?" — to several vendors and buy from the one who replies first with a clear answer. An automatic greeting buys you time. It reassures the customer instantly, even before you pick up your phone, and it keeps the conversation warm while you finish what you are doing.

Trust matters even more. Online scams have made many Nigerians cautious about sending money to strangers. A business that replies with a proper greeting, its name, its location, its opening hours and its catalogue link looks real and accountable. A business that leaves messages unread for a day looks careless. Automatic messages are not a replacement for personal replies, but they are a strong first signal that you take your business seriously.

They also save you from repeating yourself. If you sell food in Yaba, clothes in Aba, or phone accessories in Ikeja, you probably answer the same questions every day: price, sizes, colours, delivery fee, location, opening hours, payment details. A good greeting message can answer several of those questions at once, or point the customer to where the answers are, so your later replies are shorter and faster.

## How to Set Up a Greeting Message Step by Step

Setting up a greeting message takes only a few minutes. Open WhatsApp Business and tap the three dots in the top right corner on Android, or tap Settings at the bottom right on iPhone. Go to Business tools. You will see a list of business features, including Business profile, Catalogue, Away message, Greeting message, Quick replies, and Labels.

Tap Greeting message. Turn it on by tapping the toggle at the top. You will see a text box with a default message written by WhatsApp. Do not keep the default. It is generic and does not mention your business, your products or what the customer should do next. Delete it and write your own.

Write your greeting in the box. Keep it short enough to read on one phone screen. Aim for three to five lines. Mention your business name, welcome the customer, and tell them what to do next. For example: "Hello, welcome to Ada's Kitchen! Thank you for messaging us. Please tell us what you would like to order, or type MENU to see today's menu and prices. We reply within a few minutes during working hours."

Below the message box, you will see a section for recipients. Choose who receives the greeting. The usual choice is "Everyone", meaning anyone who messages you for the first time, or after fourteen days without chatting. For most small businesses, "Everyone" is correct. You want every new customer welcomed, not just people you already know.

Tap Save. That is it. Your greeting message is now live. Test it by asking a friend who has not chatted with your business number recently to send you a message. Check what they receive, how it looks, and whether any link in it works. If it looks good, leave it. If something is unclear, edit it immediately.

## How to Set Up an Away Message Step by Step

An away message is set up in a similar place. In WhatsApp Business, go to Settings, then Business tools, then tap Away message. Turn it on with the toggle.

Write your away message in the text box. Again, delete the default and write your own. A good away message has three parts: an acknowledgement, a clear statement of when you will reply, and a helpful next step. For example: "Thank you for messaging Blessing Fabrics. We are currently closed and will reply to you by 9 am tomorrow. Meanwhile, you can browse our catalogue to see our latest Ankara designs and prices. We appreciate your patience."

Next, choose your schedule. WhatsApp Business gives you three options. "Always send" means the away message goes out whenever someone messages you while the feature is turned on. Use this when you travel, take a day off, or close for a public holiday. "Custom schedule" lets you set the exact hours when the message should run, for example every day from 7 pm to 8 am. This is the best option for most businesses because it runs automatically every day without you remembering to switch it on. "Outside of business hours" uses the hours you set in your business profile, so make sure those hours are correct first.

Choose your recipients. As with greeting messages, most businesses should choose "Everyone". Do not set it to a narrow list that excludes new customers, because new customers are the ones who most need reassurance that you will reply.

Tap Save. Test it the same way: ask someone to message you outside your working hours and confirm the away message arrives as expected. Remember to turn off "Always send" when you return. A common mistake is leaving an away message on during working hours, so customers keep receiving "We are closed" at noon on a Tuesday. If you use a custom schedule, this problem disappears.

## What to Write: Greeting and Away Message Examples

The best greeting messages are specific to your business. A tailor, a food vendor, a skincare seller and a phone repairer should not use the same words. Use these templates as a starting point and replace the details with your own.

For a fashion or retail business: "Hello and welcome to [Your Business Name]! Thank you for reaching out. We sell quality ready-to-wear outfits, shoes and bags for men and women in Lagos. Please tell us what you are looking for, or tap our catalogue to see new arrivals and prices. We deliver nationwide."

For a food business: "Hello, welcome to [Your Business Name]! Thank you for messaging us. We serve fresh lunch packs, small chops and event catering in [Your Area]. Type MENU for today's menu, or tell us your order and delivery address. We reply within minutes from 8 am to 7 pm."

For a service business, such as cleaning, photography or make-up: "Hello, thank you for contacting [Your Business Name]. We offer [your service] in Lagos and Abuja. Please share what you need, your preferred date, and your location, and we will send you a quote shortly. Our working hours are Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm."

Notice what these examples have in common. They name the business. They thank the customer. They state what the business does in one line. They tell the customer exactly what to send next. They mention working hours or delivery so the customer knows what to expect. They are short. A customer reading on a small phone with limited data should understand your message in five seconds.

Away messages need to manage expectations. The customer already wants something. Your job is to hold their interest until you return. A simple closing message works well: "Thank you for messaging [Your Business Name]. We are closed for today and will reply to you first thing tomorrow morning by 9 am. Feel free to browse our catalogue or leave your order here, and we will confirm it as soon as we reopen. Thank you for your patience."

A weekend or holiday version: "Hello, thank you for reaching out. We are away for the public holiday and will be back on [date]. Your message is important to us and we will reply in the order received. For urgent enquiries, please leave a detailed message here."

Each example gives a return time. That is the most important part. "We will reply soon" is vague and frustrating. "We will reply by 9 am tomorrow" is specific and reassuring. If you cannot give an exact time, give a window, such as "within 24 hours" or "by tomorrow evening." Never promise a time you cannot keep.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many businesses set up automatic messages once and then make mistakes that reduce their effect. The first is using the default WhatsApp message. It says nothing about your business and does not guide the customer. Always write your own.

The second is making the message too long. Some owners paste their full price list, delivery terms, bank details and a long prayer into the greeting. The customer stops reading after the first two lines. Keep automatic messages short. Send details later, in a personal reply, once you know what the customer actually wants.

The third is promising what you cannot deliver. If your greeting says "We reply in five minutes" but you often take five hours, customers will feel misled. Promise a realistic time and keep it. If you are a solo founder who also goes to the market and attends to walk-in customers, "within a few hours during working hours" is honest and acceptable.

The fourth is forgetting to update messages. Prices change. You move from Yaba to Lekki. You stop selling one product and start selling another. Your opening hours change during Ramadan or December. If your greeting still mentions old prices or an old address, it creates confusion and disputes. Review your messages at the start of every month and after any major change.

## Using Quick Replies and Labels Together

Greeting and away messages work best when combined with two other WhatsApp Business tools: quick replies and labels. Quick replies are saved messages you can send with a shortcut. You can save your price list as a quick reply and send it by typing a short code. You can save your delivery fees for Lagos, Abuja and nationwide delivery, your account details, your location, and your opening hours. After the greeting message has welcomed the customer, you can answer their follow-up question in seconds without typing the same paragraph for the hundredth time.

Set up quick replies for your most common questions: price list, delivery fee, payment details, location and directions, opening hours, and how to place an order. Keep each one short and clear. Update them when prices change, just like your greeting message.

Labels help you organise customers. You can label chats as "New customer," "Pending payment," "Paid," "Delivered," or "VIP customer." This is useful when you return after an away period and have twenty unread chats. Labels help you see who has paid, who is waiting for a delivery, and who is a new enquiry, so you reply in the right order instead of replying randomly.

Together, greeting messages, away messages, quick replies and labels turn WhatsApp from a simple chat app into a small customer service system that fits in your pocket. For a business with one or two people, that is a major advantage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do greeting and away messages cost money?

No. Both features are free inside the WhatsApp Business app. You only pay for your normal data or internet connection, as you would for any WhatsApp message. There is no subscription or extra charge from WhatsApp for using them.

### Can I use greeting and away messages on regular WhatsApp?

No. These automatic messages are only available in WhatsApp Business. If you are still on regular WhatsApp, download WhatsApp Business and move your number over. It is free and your chats transfer with you.

### Will the greeting message send to everyone who messages me?

It sends to people who message you for the first time, or after fourteen days of no conversation. It does not send on every single message. That is by design, so regular customers are not greeted like strangers every day.

### Can I set different away messages for weekends and weekdays?

Yes, by using the custom schedule option. You can set the away message to run during specific hours each day, including weekends. You can also change the message text before a weekend or holiday to mention the specific return date.

### What if a customer replies to my away message?

Their reply will wait in your chat list like a normal message. When you return, reply personally and acknowledge the delay. A short apology — "Thank you for your patience, I am back now" — goes a long way.

## What to Do Next

1. Download WhatsApp Business if you have not already, and move your business number over from regular WhatsApp.
2. Complete your business profile with your correct name, address, hours, description and a clear profile photo.
3. Write your greeting message using the templates in this guide, keeping it short, warm and specific to your business.
4. Write your away message with a clear return time, and set it to a custom schedule that matches your real working hours.
5. Set up quick replies for your price list, delivery fees, payment details and location so you can reply faster.
6. Ask a friend to message your business number and check that both messages look right and any links work.
7. Add a monthly reminder to review and update your greeting, away message and quick replies so they never go out of date.

A greeting message and an away message will not sell your product for you, but they will make sure no customer feels ignored, no enquiry is lost because you were busy, and no buyer walks away thinking you do not care. For a Nigerian small business competing with many other sellers on the same phone screen, that first impression is often the difference between a sale and a missed opportunity. Set them up today, keep them updated, and let them work for you even when you are not holding your phone.
