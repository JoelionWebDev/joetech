## The honest arithmetic: what ₦1M in 90 days really looks like

Do the math backwards, with a realistic average order:

| Scenario | Timeline | Your calculation |
|----------|---------|------------------|
| Clients — services (websites, designs, video) | ₦40,000 avg per client | 5 clients/mo × 3 months = 15 clients ≈ ₦600,000 |
| Clients — mid tickets (custom dev, campaigns) | ₦100,000 avg | 3–4 big deals alone = ₦1M |
| Products (templates, ebooks, course) | ₦5–15k each | 40–60 sales total |
| Mixed (2 clients + products) | — | 2 × ₦150k + 3 × ₦25k × 3 mo ≈ ₦1M |

Notice: **no scenario needs 10,000 followers or a million views.** You need 12–15 paying relationships. That scale is achievable one person at a time.

> The trap that keeps people at ₦0 is expecting ₦1M from ONE idea. The unlock is many small "wins" per week.

## Days 1–7: Pick ONE lane and one avatar

The fastest way to earn zero is to try "everything online." Pick **one lane** from these three — the one that best matches your current skill and energy:

1. **Services lane** (fastest cash): web design, copywriting, social media management, video editing, logo/animation. Sell a *starter offer* at ₦25,000–₦90,000 for a 7-day delivery.
2. **Product lane** (best scale, slower): one digital product (a LinkedIn template pack, an ebook, a join-worthy mini-course, a Canva kit). Price ₦9,000–₦30,000, sell 20–40.
3. **Mixed lane** (the recommended): one service to bank cash in weeks 1–6, plus one product launched by week 8 to become semi-passive by month 3.

Pick ONE avatar — "small beauty/spa owners in Lagos," "founders needing pitch decks," "university students needing CVs and intros." One person types their problem into Google; you want to be the clear answer for that one type.

> Go deep on one lane for 90 days. Shallow across twenty, and nothing compounds.

## Days 8–21: Build proof in 2 weeks, not 2 months

Skip the "build a perfect portfolio for 6 months" trap — that's a hobby with a deadline. Instead:

- **Write 3 real offers** ("I redesign your Insta page + build your WhatsApp catalogue in 7 days for ₦35,000").
- **Create 3 micro-demos**: an actual free audit of one target business, sample graphics, an ebook draft — proof businesses can touch.
- **Film a 60-second video** of you explaining the offer (bonus: it increases trust by a lot).
- **Fix your basic one-page site** — simply: your name, the offer, the result, price, how to order. Even one page is enough.

Don't wait to be "perfect." In 90 days, 60 of them should be spent actively selling, not preparing to sell.

---

## Days 22–56: The outreach engine (25 conversations per week)

This is where income actually comes from. Daily routine (2 hours):

- **10 warm messages/day.** WhatsApp groups, business pages, Google Maps (your city), past classmates who run businesses, connectors in niche Facebook groups. Never copy-paste the same message — personalise the name and one detail.
- **1 value post/day** on 1 main platform (LinkedIn or Instagram or TikTok). Not "hire me" — a single tip laced with your proof. Example: "3 cheap mistakes on business Pages that push customers to competitors [link]."
- **3 helpful comments/day** — answer people's public questions about your topic with smart replies.

Run this; by day 42 you should have booked **8–12 calls** and closed **3–5 paying clients**. There's no hard "secret sauce" — the secret is that you kept going when the first 40 messages were ignored.

> Volume eats perfection. A so-so video to 60 watchers beats a masterpiece to zero.

---

## Days 43–63: Over-deliver, then auction the result

Your first clients are your resume. The name of the game now:

- **Over-deliver, quietly** — finish BEFORE the agreed date; send a small bonus (logo and polished deliverable, a free fix, a template).
- **Ask for the proof twice** — "Would you leave 2 lines I can show to similar businesses?" And ask again at day 60.
- **Replicate the style** — with permission, screenshot a positive WhatsApp review and post a mini case-study: "How [client name] got [their result] in 10 days."
- **Turn one-offs into retainers** — "I know you'll need an refresh next quarter; shall we lock in a monthly agreement?"

By week 9, 60–70% of your month can be retainers + referrals. That's the transition from "hustle" to "income."

---

## Days 64–90: Add the product (or go bigger)

With cash flow finally steady, launch the second lane for the final stretch:

- **Product idea** — a focused "how I did it" version of what you now know: "how I structured a Lagos menu," a Figma starter kit, a WhatsApp catalogue template pack, a "CV to Interview" guide. Build in 3 days max, price ₦9,000–₦25,000, sell via WhatsApp + a simple order form.

- **Or scale the service** — raise your price by 25% (you're credible now) and take one bigger client for the month.

**Social proof distribution:** ask every happy client to "share with one person who needs this." Run one "last spots this month" promotion in the final 7 days with a fast-start offer. This sprint is where momentum peaks: product + retainer + referrals compound.

| Tue–Thu | Outreach + calls + service delivery |

That's ~2 focused hours a day — realistic with a job, feasible full-time.

## The 5 traps that actually kill your progress

1. **Message blasting** — sending 200 strangers one generic message is not outreach; personalise each note, or it will underperform.
2. **Underpricing for "experience"** — keep ONE low-charge offer limited to your first 3 clients, and always in return for a testimonial.
3. **Tech overreach** — you don't need a ₦50k website first. Keep the offer in WhatsApp + Google tools until the cash flows.
4. **Sloppy payments** — collect 50% upfront or full payment via bank, Paystack or Flutterwave, and never start without it.
5. **Quitting in week 3** — most people quit at week 3. Week 4–5 is where replies start. Stay.
