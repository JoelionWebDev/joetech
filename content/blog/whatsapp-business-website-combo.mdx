If you run a business in Lagos, your sales process probably runs through WhatsApp. A customer sees your product, sends a message, gets a price list, asks a few questions, and transfers payment â€” all in one chat thread.

WhatsApp is how Nigerian businesses actually close sales. But relying on WhatsApp alone has a problem: you cannot be found on Google, you do not look professional to new customers, and you have no way to build trust before the first message.

The solution is not to replace WhatsApp. It is to put a website in front of it.

## The Problem with WhatsApp-Only

When your entire business runs through WhatsApp:

- **You have no Google presence.** Someone searching for your service will not find you unless they already know your name.
- **You look unprofessional.** A business with no website is hard to take seriously, especially for high-ticket services like real estate, legal advice, or construction.
- **You cannot build trust at scale.** Every new customer starts from zero. There is no portfolio, no testimonials, no service page to convince them you are the right choice.
- **You have no way to capture leads who are not ready to chat.** Many potential customers browse at night or during work hours when they cannot start a conversation. Without a website, you lose them.

## The Solution: Website-First, WhatsApp-Second

A website does the work your WhatsApp cannot:

1. It gets found on Google when someone searches for what you offer.
2. It makes a professional first impression with your portfolio, testimonials, and service descriptions.
3. It captures visitors who are not ready to chat â€” they can browse, read, and come back later.
4. It hands the warm lead straight into WhatsApp with one click.

The handoff is the key. Once a visitor has seen your site and is convinced, they should be able to start a WhatsApp conversation with you immediately â€” without copying a phone number, leaving the page, or typing a long message.

## How the Integration Actually Works

There are two main approaches:

### Simple Click-to-Chat Link

Every WhatsApp number has a built-in click-to-chat URL. The format is:

```
https://wa.me/2348012345678
```

You can add a pre-filled message:

```
https://wa.me/2348012345678?text=Hi%20I%20saw%20your%20website%20and%20I%20am%20interested%20in%20your%20services
```

We place this link behind a floating widget that follows the user as they scroll â€” a green button in the bottom corner of every page. One tap opens WhatsApp with your number and a custom message already filled in.

### Official WhatsApp Business API

For businesses that receive high volumes of inquiries, the WhatsApp Business API adds features like:

- Automated greeting messages when someone messages you for the first time
- Quick reply buttons for common questions
- Away messages when you are offline
- A verified business profile with your description, email, and address

The API requires approval from Meta and usually comes with a monthly fee, but for growing businesses, the professionalism and automation are worth it.
