Email marketing delivers the highest return on investment of any marketing channel. For every naira spent on email marketing, the average return is ₦3,600. No other channel comes close.

Email is direct, personal, and owned by you. Unlike social media algorithms that decide who sees your content, email reaches your subscribers directly.

Here is how to build an email marketing strategy for your Nigerian business.

## Building Your Email List

Your email list is your most valuable marketing asset. Grow it intentionally with quality subscribers who want to hear from you.

Offer a lead magnet — a free resource that provides value in exchange for an email address. Lead magnets include ebooks, guides, checklists, templates, discounts, or free consultations. The lead magnet should be relevant to your business and valuable to your audience.

Place sign-up forms strategically on your website — homepage, blog posts, landing pages, and pop-ups. Keep forms simple. Asking for just an email address increases sign-up rates. You can collect more information later.

Collect emails offline too. In-store sign-ups, business cards collected at events, and phone inquiries can all be added to your list with permission.

## Email Marketing Platforms

Email marketing platforms manage your list and send campaigns. Popular options include Mailchimp for beginners with a generous free tier, ConvertKit for creators and bloggers, Sendinblue for affordable pricing with SMS marketing, and MailerLite for simplicity and value.

Choose a platform that fits your list size, budget, and feature requirements. Most platforms offer free trials.

## Types of Email Campaigns

Welcome emails are sent when someone joins your list. Welcome emails have the highest open rates — often over eighty percent. Introduce yourself, deliver your lead magnet, and set expectations for future emails.

Newsletter emails provide regular value to your subscribers. Share blog posts, industry news, tips, and company updates. Newsletters build ongoing relationships.

Promotional emails announce sales, new products, or special offers. Use sparingly to avoid subscriber fatigue. Segment your list to send relevant promotions.

Nurture sequences are automated email series that guide subscribers toward a purchase. A typical sequence might include educational content, social proof, and an offer.

Re-engagement emails target subscribers who have not opened your emails in a while. Ask if they still want to hear from you. Remove inactive subscribers who do not respond.

## Writing Effective Emails

Write subject lines that compel opens. Subject lines should be concise, specific, and create curiosity. Avoid spammy words like free, guarantee, or act now.

Personalize your emails beyond using the subscriber's name. Segment your list and send relevant content to each segment. Behavioral targeting based on what subscribers clicked or purchased increases relevance.

Focus on your reader. Use you more than we or I. Address their needs and interests. Make every email valuable to the reader.

Include a clear call to action in every email. Tell readers exactly what you want them to do — read a blog post, download a resource, make a purchase, or share with a friend.

## Automation

Email automation sends the right message at the right time without manual effort. Set up automated sequences for new subscribers, abandoned carts, post-purchase follow-up, and re-engagement.

Automation nurtures leads while you focus on other aspects of your business. It ensures consistent communication with every subscriber.

## Measurement

Track open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe rate, and list growth rate. Benchmark your metrics against industry averages. Test subject lines, send times, and content to improve performance.
