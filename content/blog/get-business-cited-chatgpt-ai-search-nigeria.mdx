Something has changed about how Nigerians find businesses. A customer who once typed "best tailor in Lekki" into Google now asks ChatGPT, "Where can I get a good tailor in Lekki who delivers on time?" The answer they get is a short list of named businesses — and if your business is not on that list, you were never even considered.

This is AI search, and it is already shaping buying decisions in Nigeria. The good news is that getting cited is not a mystery reserved for big companies. AI tools recommend businesses they can find, understand, and trust, and this guide explains how to become one of them.

## How AI Search Chooses Businesses

When someone asks an AI tool for a recommendation, it draws on the information it was trained on and on live web results it can search in real time. From all of that, it picks the businesses that look most relevant, credible, and clearly described.

Three things matter most. First, clarity: can the AI tell exactly what you sell, who you serve, and where you operate? Second, consistency: does the same business name, service description, and location appear across your website, your Google Business Profile, and your social media pages? Third, authority: do other trustworthy pages mention you, link to you, or review you? If your website says one thing and your Instagram bio says another, an AI tool has no confident answer to give about you.

## Make Your Website Easy for AI to Understand

AI tools read websites the way a very fast, very literal reader would. They look for plain language, clear headings, and direct answers. A website full of vague slogans is hard for them to use. A website that says plainly what you do is easy.

Start with your homepage. It should state, in the first few lines, what your business does and who it is for. "Joetech builds websites for small businesses in Lagos" is far more useful to an AI tool than "We bring your dreams to life." Then create pages that answer the exact questions your customers ask, such as "How much does a website cost in Nigeria?" Use proper headings, keep paragraphs short, and add a frequently asked questions section to your main service pages. AI tools love question-and-answer formats, because that is how people ask them things.

## Keep Your Business Details Consistent Everywhere

When the same business name, address, phone number, and service description appear in the same form across the web, the AI treats your business as real, established, and trustworthy. Check your website footer and contact page, your Google Business Profile, your social media bios, and any directories or marketplaces where you are listed, and make them all match.

Use the same business name everywhere. If you are "Adaeze Hair Studio" on your website, do not be "Adaeze Hair" on Instagram and "Adaeze Hair & Beauty" on Google. The same goes for your location. Small differences create doubt, and doubt costs you citations.

Your Google Business Profile deserves special attention. It is free, it takes less than an hour to set up properly, and it is one of the sources AI tools check most often for local businesses. Fill in every field, add photos, and encourage happy customers to leave reviews. Reply to every review you receive — reviews are powerful trust signals.

## Publish Helpful Content That Answers Real Questions

AI tools cite sources that answer questions well. That means businesses that publish genuinely useful content get mentioned far more often than businesses that only publish adverts about themselves.

Think about the questions your customers ask you every week. A fashion designer hears "How do I know my size when ordering online?" A solar installer hears "How many panels do I need for a three-bedroom flat?" Each of those questions is a blog post waiting to happen, and each post is a chance to be cited. Write the answer the way you would explain it to a customer standing in front of you, with Nigerian context where it matters: prices in naira, locations in Lagos or Abuja, and delivery realities like traffic and power supply.

For a deeper look at planning this kind of content, read our guide on [content marketing strategy](/blog/content-marketing-strategy-guide). You do not need to publish every day — one solid, useful article a week beats five rushed posts.

## Earn Mentions and Links From Other Websites

An AI tool is more likely to recommend a business that other websites mention. Practical ways to earn mentions include getting listed in reputable Nigerian business directories, being featured in a local blog or industry publication, guest posting on a website your customers read, and partnering with complementary businesses who link to each other.

You can also create content that other sites naturally want to reference: a simple guide, a useful checklist, or a clear explanation of a confusing topic in your field. When a blogger needs a source, they link to the clearest explanation they can find. Make sure that explanation is yours.

## Make Sure Your Website Is Technically Sound

All the good content in the world will not help if AI tools cannot reach or read your website. Your site must load quickly, work on mobile phones, and be secure with HTTPS. Slow websites get skipped, by people and by AI crawlers alike. Most Nigerians browse on their phones, and AI tools check the mobile version of your site first. A website without HTTPS looks unsafe, and AI tools are cautious about recommending unsafe sites.

Your site must also be easy to crawl. If your pages are hidden behind logins or broken links, search engines and AI crawlers cannot read them. A simple sitemap helps crawlers find every page.

## What Not to Do

Do not stuff your pages with keywords in unnatural sentences, and do not copy content from other websites — original content written for your own customers is what gets cited. Do not invent claims about your business, such as awards you have not won or results you cannot show. AI tools cross-check claims against multiple sources. And do not pay anyone who promises to "guarantee" a ChatGPT citation. No one can guarantee that. Do the work in this guide, and let the citations follow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to get cited by ChatGPT?

There is no fixed timeline. Some businesses see mentions within weeks of publishing clear, helpful content and fixing their online listings. For most small businesses, expect a few months of consistent work before citations become regular.

### Do I need to pay ChatGPT to be recommended?

No. ChatGPT does not sell recommendations, and paying for a subscription does not make the tool recommend your business. Citations come from the quality and clarity of your public online presence.

### Does AI search replace Google SEO?

Not yet. Many Nigerians still search on Google, and Google itself now shows AI-generated answers at the top of results. The good news is that the work overlaps: clear content, a fast website, consistent details, and genuine reviews help you in both places at the same time.

### Can a small business compete with big brands in AI search?

Yes. AI tools value specific, direct answers to specific questions. A small business that answers "How much does a borehole cost in Ibadan?" clearly and honestly can outrank a big brand that has never written about it. Local, specific, honest content is your edge.

## Start With the Basics Today

Getting cited by ChatGPT and AI search is not a trick. It is the result of doing the fundamentals well: a clear website, consistent business details, helpful content that answers real questions, and a reputation other websites are willing to mention. Start with the easiest wins — your Google Business Profile and your homepage wording — then build the habit of publishing one useful article at a time.
