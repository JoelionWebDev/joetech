Paid video advertising delivers the highest return on investment of any digital advertising format. Video ads capture attention, communicate emotion, and drive action more effectively than static images or text.

But video ads are expensive to produce and run. A poorly made video ad wastes both production budget and advertising spend.

Here is how to create video ads that sell for your Nigerian business.

## Types of Video Ads

In-stream ads play before, during, or after other video content on YouTube or connected TV. Viewers can skip after five seconds. The first five seconds are critical for hooking attention.

In-feed ads appear in social media feeds as native content. They blend with organic content but are marked as sponsored. These work well for awareness and engagement goals.

Stories ads appear between Instagram and Facebook stories. They are full-screen vertical format and last up to fifteen seconds. They are immersive and high-engagement.

Discovery ads appear in YouTube search results and alongside related videos. Viewers choose to click, so the thumbnail and title must compel action.

## Structure of a High-Converting Video Ad

The first five seconds must hook the viewer. Start with the problem, a provocative question, or a compelling visual. Viewers decide whether to continue watching within seconds.

The next ten to fifteen seconds present the solution. Show your product or service addressing the problem. Focus on the benefit, not the features. Show how life improves after using your product.

The last five seconds deliver the call to action. Tell viewers exactly what to do — visit your website, use a promo code, sign up for a free trial, or make a purchase. Make the action simple and immediate.

## Targeting

Platform targeting options vary. Facebook and Instagram offer detailed demographic, interest, and behavior targeting. YouTube targets based on search intent and viewing history. LinkedIn targets by job title, industry, and company size.

Retargeting is the most effective targeting strategy. Show video ads to people who have already visited your website or engaged with your content. These warm audiences convert at higher rates.

Custom audiences can be built from your email list, website visitors, or customer data. Upload your contact list to Facebook or Google to target existing leads and customers.

Lookalike audiences find new people similar to your existing customers. Upload your customer list and platforms find users with similar characteristics and behaviors.

## Budget and Bidding

Start with a small test budget to validate your video ad creative and targeting. Run tests for at least a week before scaling.

Optimize for the action that matters most to your business. Conversions are more valuable than views. If you want sales, optimize for purchases rather than video views.

Cost per result varies by platform, targeting, and competition. Nigerian audiences are generally less expensive to reach than US or European audiences. Test different bid strategies to find what works for your market.

## Creative Best Practices

Use square or vertical video for social media ads. Vertical video fills the mobile screen and drives higher engagement. Square video performs well in feed placements.

Include captions because many viewers watch without sound. Captions increase completion rates and make your ad accessible.

Show your product or service early. Viewers should see what you are selling within the first few seconds. Do not bury the product in a long story.

Use social proof. Testimonials, reviews, and user count statistics build trust within the ad format. Viewers seeing others use your product are more likely to convert.

## Measuring Success

Track view-through rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, and cost per acquisition. Compare these metrics across different ad creative, targeting, and platforms.

Use UTM parameters to track which videos drive website traffic and conversions. This attribution data helps you optimize future campaigns.
