Scroll through Instagram or Facebook for one minute and you will notice something: the businesses that get likes, comments and messages are usually the ones with attractive posts. A clean design, a clear message and a good photo make people stop scrolling. The businesses with blurry photos and crowded text get ignored — no matter how good their products are.

The good news is that you do not need to hire a graphic designer or learn complicated software to make posts that look professional. Canva is a free design tool that runs in your browser or on your phone, and it was built for people with no design experience. With ready-made templates and simple drag-and-drop tools, you can create a post for your business in ten minutes.

This guide shows you, step by step, how to use Canva to design social media posts for your business — from setting up your account to choosing colours, writing text that sells, and posting consistently without spending hours every day.

## Getting Started with Canva

Canva works on your phone and on your computer. For most beginners, the phone app is the easiest place to start, because you can design and post from the same device. Download the Canva app from the Play Store or App Store, or visit canva.com on your computer and create a free account with your email or Google account.

The free version of Canva is enough for most small businesses. It gives you thousands of templates, free photos, fonts and design elements. There is a paid version called Canva Pro with more features, but you do not need it to start. Begin with the free plan and upgrade later only if you find yourself needing the extra templates or the background remover.

Once you are signed in, take five minutes to set up your brand. Canva lets you save your brand colours, your logo and your fonts in one place, so every design you make looks like it belongs to the same business. On the free plan, you can do this simply by keeping a note of your colours and reusing them, or by creating a template design that you copy each time. If you do not have a logo yet, you can design a simple one in Canva and refine it as your business grows.

Think about your colours before you design your first post. Pick two or three colours that suit your business and use them consistently — a food business might use warm reds and yellows, a fashion brand black and gold. Consistency is what makes people recognise your posts before they even read your business name.

## Choosing the Right Template and Size

Every social media platform has its own best size for posts, and Canva handles this for you. When you create a new design, type what you need in the search bar — "Instagram post", "Facebook post", "WhatsApp status", "flyer" — and Canva opens a blank canvas in the correct size, with hundreds of templates to choose from.

For Instagram and Facebook feed posts, the square format (1080 by 1080 pixels) works well. For stories and WhatsApp status, use the tall vertical format (1080 by 1920 pixels). For a Facebook cover or a banner, choose the wide format. Using the right size matters: a post designed for the wrong size gets cropped, and your text or prices can be cut off.

Do not just pick the first template you see. Search for templates using words that match your business — "food menu", "fashion sale", "real estate", "salon", "skincare", "event flyer". You will find designs made for businesses like yours. Pick one with a layout you like, then change the colours, photos and text to match your brand — that is what makes the design yours.

## Designing a Post That Stops the Scroll

A good social media post does three things in about three seconds: it catches the eye, it says one clear thing, and it tells the viewer what to do next. Here is how to achieve that in Canva.

**Start with a strong photo.** Use your own photos whenever you can — your real products, your real shop, your real food. Customers trust real photos far more than stock images. Take photos in good daylight, against a clean background, and upload them to Canva. If you must use stock photos, Canva has a large free library; search for images that look like your business and your customers.

**Keep the headline short.** Your headline is the biggest text on the design. It should be five words or fewer: "Weekend Sale — 20% Off", "Fresh Cakes, Baked Daily", "New Arrivals Just Landed". Put the details — prices, dates, location — in smaller text below, or in the caption of your post. A design crowded with words is hard to read on a phone screen.

**Leave space.** Beginners often fill every corner of a design. Resist that. Empty space around your text and photo makes the design look clean and professional, and it helps the important words stand out. If your design looks crowded, remove something rather than adding more.

**Add your contact details.** Every business post should make it easy for a customer to reach you. Add your WhatsApp number or your business name at the bottom of the design, in a size people can actually read.

## Writing Text That Gets Customers to Act

Design gets attention; words get the sale. Before you write a word on your design, decide on the one action you want the viewer to take. Do you want them to send you a WhatsApp message? Visit your shop? Order before Friday? Every post should have one clear call to action, not three.

Write like you talk. Nigerian customers respond to simple, friendly language, not formal corporate English. "Send us a DM to order" works better than "Kindly place your order via our direct messaging channel." Short sentences. Clear prices where possible. And always include the next step — a WhatsApp number, a link, or an instruction like "Tap the link in our bio."

Use the caption to do the heavy lifting. The design carries the headline; the caption carries the story, the price list, the delivery details and the call to action. Keep your caption short and scannable — a few lines, not a long essay. End every caption with one clear instruction, such as "WhatsApp us on 0800 000 0000 to order."

Be careful with claims. Do not write "best in Nigeria" or "number one" unless you can back it up, and do not copy another business's words or designs. Original, honest posts build trust, and trust is what turns followers into paying customers.

## Creating a Week of Posts in One Sitting

Consistency beats perfection. A business that posts three times a week with simple, clear designs will outperform a business that posts one perfect design a month. The secret is batching: design several posts in one sitting instead of starting from zero every day.

Start by planning your week. Pick three or four types of posts and rotate them: a product showcase, a price or promotion, a customer review, a behind-the-scenes photo, and an educational tip about your product. Write the text for all of them first, then open Canva and design them one after another. Because you are reusing your colours, fonts and layout, each design after the first takes only a few minutes.

Save your best designs as templates. In Canva, you can duplicate a design and change only the photo and the headline. This is how busy business owners keep a consistent look without starting over each time. Over a few weeks, you will build a small library of templates for sales, new arrivals, testimonials and announcements — and posting becomes a ten-minute job.

Finally, pay attention to what works. After a few weeks, look at which posts got the most likes, comments, shares and messages, and do more of that kind of post. If product photos with prices outperform plain announcements, make more of those. Let your customers' reactions guide your designs, not guesswork.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Canva really free for business use?

Yes, the free version of Canva is enough for most small businesses. It includes thousands of templates, free photos, fonts and design elements. Canva Pro adds extra features like background removal and scheduling, but you can run your social media with the free plan for a long time before needing it.

### Can I use Canva on my phone, or do I need a computer?

You can do everything on your phone with the Canva app, and many Nigerian business owners design entirely on their phones. A computer gives you a bigger screen, which some people find easier, but it is not required. Your designs sync between your phone and computer when you sign in with the same account.

### How do I add my own logo and photos to Canva?

Tap or click "Uploads" in Canva, then upload your logo and product photos from your phone or computer. Once uploaded, you can drag them into any design. Save your logo in your uploads so you can add it to every post quickly, and keep your brand colours noted down so your designs always match.

Start with one simple post today, keep your look consistent, and let your customers' reactions show you what to make more of.
