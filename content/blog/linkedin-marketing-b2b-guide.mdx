LinkedIn is the most effective platform for B2B marketing. 80% of B2B leads come from LinkedIn, and decision-makers are 6x more likely to engage on LinkedIn than any other platform.

Here is a complete guide to generating leads and building authority on LinkedIn.

## Optimise Your Profile

Your profile is your landing page. Optimise it for search and credibility.

### Profile Photo

Professional headshot with good lighting. Profiles with photos get 21x more views.

### Headline

Not your job title — your value proposition.

- Bad: "CEO at XYZ Tech"
- Good: "Helping Nigerian SMEs grow with custom web solutions 🚀 Web Dev | Branding | Digital Strategy"

### About Section

Tell your story, showcase results, and include a clear call to action.

### Featured Section

Pin your best content: case studies, portfolio pieces, testimonials.

## Content Strategy

### What to Post

- **Educational content** — Tips, how-tos, industry insights (40%)
- **Social proof** — Case studies, testimonials, client results (25%)
- **Personal stories** — Lessons learned, behind-the-scenes (20%)
- **Engagement** — Questions, polls, opinions (15%)

### Posting Frequency

Post 3-5 times per week consistently. Quality over quantity.

### Content Formats

- **Text posts** — High engagement, easy to consume
- **Carousel documents** — PDF-style posts with multiple slides
- **Video** — Native LinkedIn video gets priority in the algorithm
- **Polls** — Quick engagement boost

## Networking and Outreach

### Connect Strategically

Send personalised connection requests. Mention why you want to connect:

"Hi [Name], I enjoyed your post about [topic]. I help [industry] businesses with [service] and would love to connect."

### Engage Authentically

Comment thoughtfully on posts in your network. Add value, do not pitch.

### Direct Outreach

When reaching out to prospects:
1. Reference something specific about them
2. Explain why you are reaching out
3. Offer value, not a sales pitch
4. Make the ask small and specific

## LinkedIn Ads

### Sponsored Content

Promote your best organic posts to a targeted audience.

### Message Ads

Direct messages to targeted prospects. Must be highly personalised.

### Lead Gen Forms

Ads with pre-filled forms reduce friction for conversions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much time should I spend on LinkedIn daily?**

30 minutes minimum: 10 minutes engaging, 10 minutes creating content, 10 minutes networking.

**Should I post on weekends?**

LinkedIn engagement peaks Tuesday-Thursday mid-morning. Weekends have lower engagement but less competition.

**How do I measure LinkedIn success?**

Track: profile views, connection growth, content engagement, inbound messages, lead conversions.
