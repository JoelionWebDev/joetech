Your website has thirty seconds to explain what you do and convince visitors to stay. Text alone rarely works. A well-made explainer video can do the job in sixty seconds.

Explainer videos boost conversion rates because they communicate value quickly and emotionally. People understand your product faster when they see it in action. They connect with your brand when they hear your story.

Here is how to create explainer videos that actually convert for your Nigerian business.

## The Proven Structure

Every effective explainer video follows a simple structure. First, state the problem your customer faces. Make it real and relatable. Your audience needs to feel that you understand their struggle.

Second, introduce your solution. Show how your product or service solves the problem you just described. This is where you present your value proposition. Keep it clear and avoid jargon.

Third, show the transformation. Paint a picture of what life looks like after using your solution. The before-and-after contrast is powerful. People buy outcomes, not features.

Fourth, end with a clear call to action. Tell viewers exactly what to do next — visit your website, sign up for a free trial, call your sales team, or place an order.

## Ideal Length

Keep your explainer video between sixty and ninety seconds. That is enough time to make your case without losing attention.

Videos under thirty seconds feel rushed and rarely communicate enough value. Videos over two minutes see significant drop-off in viewership. Viewers decide within the first ten seconds whether to keep watching.

If you have more to say, make a series of short videos rather than one long video.

## Script Tips

Start with your audience's pain point, not your company history. Nobody cares when you were founded. They care about whether you can solve their problem. Open with a statement that resonates with their current struggle.

Use simple language. Avoid industry jargon. Write for a twelve-year-old to understand. Complex language confuses viewers and reduces conversion.

Read your script aloud. If it sounds unnatural spoken, rewrite it. Conversational language works better in video than formal writing.

Keep sentences short and punchy. Short sentences are easier to follow in video. Each sentence should communicate one idea.

Make every word count. In sixty seconds of video, you have about 150 words. Every word must earn its place.

## Visual Approach

Live action works well for service businesses where personality matters. Show your team, your office, your customers. Build trust through authenticity.

Animation works well for complex products or services that are difficult to film. Abstract concepts, software interfaces, and technical processes benefit from animated visualization.

Screen recording works for software products. Show your application in action with voiceover explaining what is happening.

## Production Quality

Audio quality matters more than video quality. Viewers forgive imperfect visuals but will not watch a video with bad audio. Invest in a decent microphone. Record in a quiet space. Use background music at low volume.

Keep visuals simple. Cluttered visuals distract from your message. Use clean graphics, simple animations, and plenty of white space.

Add captions. Many viewers watch videos without sound, especially on mobile. Captions ensure your message is communicated regardless of how the video is viewed.
