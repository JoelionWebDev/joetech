When someone searches for a business like yours in your city, Google shows local results at the top of the search page. These local results include a map and three business listings. Being in these top three results can dramatically increase your visibility and customers.

Google My Business (GMB) is the tool that controls how your business appears in these local search results and on Google Maps.

Here is how to optimize your Google My Business listing for your Nigerian business.

## Setting Up Your Listing

Create or claim your Google My Business listing at business.google.com. You need a Google account to get started. Verify your business by postcard, phone, or email. Verification proves you are the legitimate business owner.

Complete every field in your profile. Incomplete profiles rank lower than complete ones. Fill in your business name, address, phone number, website URL, business hours, category, and description.

## Optimization Best Practices

Choose the most specific category for your business. The category helps Google understand what you do and who to show your listing to. You can select multiple categories if relevant.

Write a compelling business description. Include relevant keywords that customers might search for. Describe what you offer and why customers should choose you. Keep it concise and informative.

Add high-quality photos of your business. Listings with photos receive more clicks and engagement. Include photos of your exterior, interior, team, products, and services. Update photos regularly.

Select the attributes that apply to your business. Attributes include free Wi-Fi, wheelchair accessible, outdoor seating, and appointment required. These help customers find businesses that meet their needs.

## Managing Reviews

Reviews significantly impact your local ranking and customer decisions. Encourage satisfied customers to leave reviews. Make it easy by sending them a direct link to your review page.

Respond to all reviews — positive and negative. Thank customers for positive reviews. Address negative reviews professionally and offer to resolve the issue. Responding shows you value customer feedback.

Never buy fake reviews or ask only for positive reviews. Google penalizes businesses that manipulate reviews.

## Posts and Updates

Google My Business allows you to create posts that appear in your listing. Posts can promote events, offers, products, or share updates. Posts expire after seven days but remain visible in your profile.

Post regularly to keep your listing active. Active listings rank higher than inactive ones. Share news, promotions, and helpful content through posts.

## Insights and Analytics

Google My Business provides insights about how customers find and interact with your listing. Track how customers find your listing — direct search (searching your business name) or discovery search (searching a category or service).

Track customer actions including website clicks, phone calls, direction requests, and photo views. Use this data to understand what drives customer engagement.

## Common Mistakes

Inconsistent business information across the web hurts your local ranking. Ensure your name, address, and phone number are identical on your website, GMB, and all other directories.

Using a PO box or virtual address instead of a physical location. Google requires a physical address for most business categories. Use your actual business location.

Neglecting your listing. An incomplete or outdated listing ranks lower. Keep your information current and respond to reviews.
