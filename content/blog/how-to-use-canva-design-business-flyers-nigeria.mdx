Walk through any market in Lagos, or scroll through WhatsApp status for five minutes, and you will see hundreds of flyers. Some stop you immediately — clear headline, good photo, price you can read at a glance. Most do not. They are crowded, the text is tiny, the colours clash, and you cannot tell what is being sold or how to order. The difference is rarely money. It is design, and design is now something you can do yourself, for free, on your phone.

Canva is a free design app that runs in your browser and on Android and iPhone. It gives you ready-made templates, fonts, colours and photos, and lets you change them with taps and drags — no design training, no laptop, no expensive software. With a little practice, a food vendor in Surulere, a fashion seller in Lekki, a tutor in Ikeja or a caterer in Abuja can make flyers that look like they came from a professional studio.

This guide shows you how, step by step: setting up Canva, choosing the right size, designing a flyer that actually sells, and exporting it for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and print. By the end, you will be able to make a clean flyer in under thirty minutes.

## Step 1: Choose the Right Flyer Size First

The most common beginner mistake is designing a flyer at the wrong size and then wondering why it looks blurry or cropped on WhatsApp. Decide where the flyer will be seen before you design it.

Here are the sizes to use:

- **WhatsApp status and Instagram story:** 1080 by 1920 pixels — tall, full-screen, 9:16. This is the size most Nigerian customers will see your flyer at, because they watch status every morning.
- **Instagram or Facebook post:** 1080 by 1080 pixels — square. Good for your feed, for WhatsApp groups, and for forwarding.
- **Printed flyer (A5):** use Canva's A5 template. If a printer in Computer Village or Ikeja asks for a size, A5 is the small handout size, and A4 is the larger one. Design at the print size, not at a phone size, or your print will come out blurry.
- **WhatsApp display or catalogue image:** square, 1080 by 1080, works well.

In Canva, tap "Create a design" and search for the size you want — "Instagram post", "WhatsApp status", or "Flyer". Pick the template size, not a random blank page. Designing at the correct size from the start saves you from resizing everything later.

One practical tip for Nigeria: many customers view flyers on small, low-cost Android phones with dim screens and slow data. Design for that reality. Big text, strong contrast, and a clear photo will be seen. Tiny text, pale colours and busy backgrounds will not.

## Step 2: Start From a Template — Then Make It Yours

You do not need to start from a blank page. Canva has thousands of flyer templates for food, fashion, beauty, real estate, tutoring, events and more. Search for your business type — "food flyer", "fashion sale", "salon flyer", "church programme" — and browse until one feels close to what you want.

Pick a template with a simple layout: one strong photo area, one clear headline, and space for a few lines of text. Avoid templates with ten text boxes, five colours and decorations everywhere. Simple templates are easier to edit and easier for customers to read.

Then make it yours. Change the headline to your words. Replace the template photo with your own product photo — your actual food, your actual dress, your actual shop. Change the colours to your brand colours, or to colours that suit your business. Replace the contact details with yours. A template is a starting point, not a finished flyer. The sellers whose flyers look generic are the ones who change only the phone number and leave everything else as Canva made it.

## Step 3: Write Words That Sell — Short, Clear, Honest

A flyer is not a story. A customer gives it two seconds on WhatsApp status before swiping. Your words must work in those two seconds.

Every good business flyer needs five things, and only five:

1. **A headline that says what you sell.** "Fresh Ofada Rice — Cooked to Order in Lekki" is clear. "Welcome to our page" is not a headline. Put the product or the benefit first, in the biggest text on the flyer.
2. **One or two lines of detail.** What is included, what sizes are available, or what makes it special. "Available in small, medium and large. Delivery across Lagos." That is enough. Do not write a paragraph on a flyer.
3. **The price, clearly.** Nigerians want the price. A flyer without a price gets fewer orders, because many customers will not message you to ask — they will move to the next seller who shows the price. Write "₦15,000" in large, readable numbers. If you have several prices, show the starting price: "From ₦10,000".
4. **How to order.** Your WhatsApp number, written clearly, and your location. "Order on WhatsApp: 0803 123 4567. Lekki Phase 1." If you deliver, say so. If customers can walk in, give the address or a landmark.
5. **Your business name and logo.** Small, at the top or the bottom, so people remember who you are and can find you again.

Keep the total word count low — aim for under thirty words on the whole flyer, plus your contact details. Every extra word makes the flyer harder to read on a phone. If you need to explain more, put the explanation in the WhatsApp caption or message that goes with the flyer, not on the flyer itself.

Use honest words. If it is "fresh", it must be fresh. If it is "original", it must be original. Nigerian customers have been disappointed by exaggerated claims before, and one dishonest flyer can cost you the trust that brings repeat orders.

## Step 4: Choose Colours and Fonts That People Can Read

Colour is what makes a customer stop scrolling. It is also what makes a flyer unreadable when it is done badly. Two rules will keep you safe: use few colours, and make the text contrast strongly with the background.

Pick two main colours and one accent colour, and use the same ones on every flyer. If your logo is blue and white, your flyers should be mostly blue and white. If you sell food, warm colours like orange, red and yellow make food look appetising. If you sell skincare or beauty, soft colours like pink, cream and gold feel calm and premium. Consistency matters — after a few flyers, customers will recognise your colours before they read your name.

Never put light text on a light background, or dark text on a dark photo, without a solid block behind the text. Yellow text on a white background is invisible. White text on a busy market photo is unreadable. If your photo is busy, add a solid colour rectangle behind the text, or darken the photo slightly, so the words stand out. Canva lets you do both with a few taps.

For fonts, use at most two on one flyer: one bold font for the headline and one simple font for the details. Canva has many fonts, but most are decorative and hard to read. Safe choices include Montserrat, Poppins, Open Sans and Lato for clean, modern flyers. Avoid script fonts for prices and phone numbers — a phone number in a curly font is a phone number nobody can read, and a lost sale.

Make the headline the biggest text, the price the second biggest, and everything else smaller. This order tells the customer's eye where to look first, second and third. If everything is the same size, nothing stands out, and the customer reads nothing.

## Step 5: Use Your Own Photos — Good Ones

A flyer lives or dies by its photo. Customers buy what they can see, and in Nigeria, where many sellers use stock photos that look nothing like the real product, your own honest photo is a competitive advantage.

You do not need a professional camera. A modern phone camera is enough if you follow a few basics. Take photos in daylight, near a window or outdoors in the morning, not under a yellow bulb at night. Use a clean background — a plain wall, a clean table, a white bedsheet. Wipe the lens before you shoot. Take five or six shots and pick the sharpest one.

For food, photograph the food itself, close up, with steam or fresh garnish visible. For clothes, photograph them on a person or a hanger against a plain wall, not crumpled on a bed. For hair, show the hair worn, in good light. For a service like cleaning or repairs, show a clear before-and-after. Real photos build trust in a way that downloaded images never do.

In Canva, upload your photo and place it in the largest area of the flyer. Do not stretch it — stretching makes people and products look distorted. Use Canva's crop tool to fit the photo to the space. If the photo is dark, raise the brightness slightly. If you need to remove the background from a product photo, that is a Pro feature, but a clean photo on a plain background often looks just as good for free.

## Step 6: Lay Out the Flyer So the Eye Knows Where to Go

Good layout is invisible — the customer simply finds what she needs without thinking. Bad layout makes her work, and she will not work for a flyer. She will swipe past it.

Follow a simple order from top to bottom. At the top, your business name or logo, small. Below it, the big headline. Below that, the photo. Below the photo, the price and the key details. At the bottom, how to order and your phone number. This top-to-bottom flow matches how Nigerians read a phone screen, and it works for almost every business flyer.

Leave empty space. Beginners fill every corner of a flyer because empty space feels wasted. In design, empty space is what makes the important parts stand out. If your headline touches the edge of the flyer, add a margin. If your text boxes crowd each other, move them apart. A flyer with breathing room looks professional; a flyer packed to the edges looks desperate.

Align your text. In Canva, use the alignment guides that appear as you drag — they help you line up text boxes neatly. Centre the headline, or align everything to the left. Do not mix centred and left-aligned text randomly on the same flyer. Neat alignment is one of the fastest ways to make a free design look expensive.

Check the flyer on your phone before you send it. What looks fine on a laptop can be unreadable on a phone. Zoom out in Canva, or download the flyer and view it in your gallery at normal size. Can you read the price without zooming? Can you read the phone number? Is the headline clear in two seconds? If not, make the text bigger and remove something. When in doubt, remove. A flyer with three clear messages beats a flyer with ten messages nobody reads.

## Step 7: Export, Share and Print Without Losing Quality

When your flyer looks right, download it correctly. In Canva, tap Share, then Download. For WhatsApp and Instagram, choose PNG — it keeps text sharp. For printing, choose PDF Print, which gives the printer the best quality. Do not screenshot your flyer from Canva and send the screenshot; screenshots are lower quality and often include parts of the app screen.

Keep your file size sensible for Nigerian data realities. A PNG flyer of one or two megabytes is fine for WhatsApp. If Canva gives you a very large file, you can reduce the size slightly when downloading, but do not compress it so much that the text goes blurry. Test by sending the flyer to your own second number or a family group first, and open it on a phone to check it still looks sharp.

When sharing on WhatsApp, add a short caption with the flyer, not the same words repeated. For example: "New Ankara styles just arrived — from ₦25,000. Send a DM to order, delivery across Lagos." The caption gives context and a call to action, while the flyer gives the visual. Post it on your status, in your broadcast list if the people asked to receive updates, and in groups where such posts are allowed. Do not spam groups that forbid adverts — you will be removed, and your business name will be remembered for the wrong reason.

For print, save the PDF and take it to a printer on a flash drive or send it on WhatsApp. Ask the printer for a sample print before they print fifty copies, and check the colours and the text size on paper. Colours on a screen are brighter than colours on paper, so a flyer that looks vivid on your phone may look duller in print. A test print saves you from wasting money.

Name your files clearly so you can find them later: "ankara-sale-nov-2026.png" is useful; "IMG-4829.png" is not. Keep your Canva designs organised in folders by month or by campaign, so you can reuse a good layout next time instead of starting from scratch.

## Common Flyer Mistakes Nigerian Sellers Make

- **Too much text.** A flyer is not a price list. If you have ten products, make ten flyers or a simple catalogue, not one flyer with ten tiny prices.
- **No price.** Customers assume a missing price means an expensive price. Show it, or at least show "From ₦…".
- **A blurry photo.** A dark, blurry or stretched photo tells the customer the product may be poor too. Retake the photo in daylight.
- **Too many fonts and colours.** Three fonts and six colours on one flyer looks chaotic. Two fonts and two or three colours look professional.
- **A phone number that cannot be read.** Check the number digit by digit before you post. One wrong digit sends your customers to a stranger.

## A Simple Weekly Flyer Routine

Designing flyers should become a habit, not a crisis each time you have something to sell. A simple weekly routine keeps your marketing steady without taking over your day.

- **Monday:** decide what you want to promote this week — one product, one offer, or one service. Do not promote everything at once.
- **Tuesday:** take fresh photos in daylight and pick the best one. Write the headline, price and ordering details in your notes app first, so you are not writing on the flyer itself.
- **Wednesday:** design the flyer in Canva, using your saved template. Check it on your phone, fix the text size, and download it as PNG.
- **Thursday:** post it on WhatsApp status, your Instagram feed, and Facebook. Send it to your broadcast list if the offer is genuinely useful to them.
- **Friday:** check the response. How many people asked about it? How many ordered? Keep the designs that brought orders, and note what worked — the photo, the price, or the headline — so you can repeat it.

Over a month, you will have four flyers, and you will be faster at each one. After three months, you will have a small library of designs you can reuse and adapt, and flyer design will take minutes instead of hours.

## Final Word

You do not need to hire a designer for every flyer, and you do not need to accept flyers that look rushed or unreadable. Canva gives you the tools for free, on the phone you already own. What it cannot give you is the judgement: a clear headline, an honest photo, a readable price, and the discipline to keep it simple. That part is yours, and it is what separates a flyer that sells from a flyer that is ignored.

Start today. Open Canva, pick one template for your best product, replace the photo with your own, write a short headline and a clear price, and post it on your status this evening. Then do it again next week, a little better. In a month, your flyers will look like a brand — because they will be one.
