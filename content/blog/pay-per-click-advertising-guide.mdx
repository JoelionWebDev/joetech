Pay-per-click (PPC) advertising lets you appear at the top of Google search results immediately. Unlike SEO, which takes months to build, PPC delivers traffic as soon as your campaign is approved.

You pay only when someone clicks your ad. This makes PPC measurable and controllable. You know exactly what you are paying for each visitor.

Here is how to get started with Google Ads for your Nigerian business.

## How Google Ads Works

Google Ads is an auction system. Advertisers bid on keywords relevant to their business. When someone searches for a keyword you are bidding on, Google runs an auction to determine which ads appear and in what order.

The auction considers your bid amount and your quality score. Quality score measures how relevant your ad and landing page are to the search query. A higher quality score means you pay less per click and rank higher.

## Keyword Research for PPC

Keyword research for PPC is different from SEO. You are looking for keywords with commercial intent — people who are ready to buy or take action.

Use Google Keyword Planner to find keywords with search volume and competition data. Look for keywords with high commercial intent. Web developer in Lagos has higher purchase intent than how to build a website.

Negative keywords prevent your ads from showing for irrelevant searches. If you sell premium web development services, add free or cheap as negative keywords.

## Campaign Structure

Organize your account into campaigns and ad groups. Each campaign should have a specific goal and budget. Each ad group within a campaign should focus on a theme of related keywords.

A typical structure might have campaigns for brand keywords, non-brand keywords, competitor keywords, and remarketing. Each campaign has ad groups for specific product or service categories.

## Ad Copy

Your ad copy must convince searchers to click your ad instead of competitors' ads. Include your keyword in the headline. Highlight your unique value proposition. Include a call to action.

Write multiple ad variations for each ad group. Google tests different versions and shows the best performers more often.

Ad extensions add extra information to your ads — phone numbers, location, site links, and reviews. Extensions improve click-through rates and provide more reasons to choose your business.

## Landing Pages

The page people land on after clicking your ad determines whether they convert. Your landing page must deliver what your ad promises. If your ad offers Web Development in Lagos, the landing page should be about web development in Lagos.

Optimize landing pages for conversion. Clear headline, compelling copy, strong call to action, minimal distractions, and fast loading speed.

## Budget and Bidding

Start with a small daily budget you are comfortable spending. Typical Nigerian businesses start with ₦5,000 to ₦20,000 per day.

Choose your bidding strategy based on your goal. Maximize clicks drives traffic. Target CPA drives conversions at a specific cost. Manual CPC gives you full control over bids.

Monitor your campaigns daily during the first few weeks. Adjust bids, pause underperforming keywords, and test new ad copy.

## Measurement

Track impressions, clicks, click-through rate, cost per click, conversions, conversion rate, and cost per acquisition. Use Google Ads conversion tracking and Google Analytics.

Calculate your return on ad spend. If you spend ₦100,000 on ads and generate ₦400,000 in revenue, your ROAS is 4:1.
