## Step 2: Create a Lead Magnet

A lead magnet is a free resource you offer in exchange for an email address.

### Effective Lead Magnets
- PDF guide or checklist
- Email course (5-7 lessons delivered over 2 weeks)
- Template or swipe file
- Discount code or coupon
- Webinar or video training

### What Makes a Good Lead Magnet
- Solves a specific problem
- Delivers immediate value
- Easy to consume (not a 100-page ebook)
- Related to what you sell

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many subscribers do I need to start seeing results?**

500 engaged subscribers can generate consistent leads and sales if you nurture them well. Quality matters more than quantity.

**Should I buy an email list?**

No. Bought lists have high bounce rates, damage your sender reputation, and violate anti-spam laws. Build your list organically.

**How often should I email my list?**

Weekly for most businesses. Bi-weekly is acceptable. Monthly is too infrequent to build a relationship.
